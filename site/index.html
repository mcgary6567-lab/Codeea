Frequently Asked Questions

What is Prometheus?
Prometheus is an automated crypto trading bot platform. You configure a strategy once, connect your exchange, and the bot handles execution for you — 24/7, hands-free.
Do I need trading experience to use it?
No. Prometheus is built for beginners, with guided setup and ready-made strategy presets. You can also start in demo mode to learn the platform before going live.
How does it connect to my exchange?
You link your exchange account through a secure API key. Your funds stay in your own exchange account the entire time — the bot can place trades but cannot withdraw your money.
Which exchanges are supported?
Popular exchanges including Binance, Coinbase, Kraken, KuCoin, OKX, Bitget, Gate, HTX, Crypto.com and more. See the full list in the "Connect All Your Exchanges" section above.
Which operating systems does it run on?
Prometheus is a desktop app for Windows and macOS. Your licence works on either — install it on whichever computer you use.
Is there a demo mode?
Yes. Demo (paper) mode lets you test strategies in a simulated environment with no real funds at risk, so you can get comfortable before activating a live account.
How long does setup take?
Most users are up and running in around 10 minutes. If you'd like help, the Done-For-You setup bonus includes an expert call to configure everything with you.
Is there a refund policy?
Yes — a 90-day money-back guarantee on your purchase. Full terms are on our Refund Policy page.
