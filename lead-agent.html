How it works

Built to protect your accounts

Careful by design

Lead Agent sends the way a person would, so your Gmail and your reputation stay safe.

  • Warm-up: 10 emails a day at first, rising each week, with a daily cap you set
  • Random gaps between emails, sent in each lead's own daytime
  • Stops by itself after repeated failures or too many bounces
  • "Unsubscribe" replies go on a do-not-contact list, for good
  • Never answers replies by itself: you read, check and send
  • Pauses on CAPTCHAs and stops if a site blocks it; it never tries to get around them

Before you buy

What you need, honestly

Lead Agent runs inside your browser, not in the cloud. That keeps your data private, and it means a few things are up to you.

You need

  • A computer with Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi or Firefox 128+
  • The browser open while it collects and sends (it pauses when the computer is off and carries on later)
  • A Gmail account signed in to that browser, for sending
  • Your own postal address for the bottom of your emails (required by anti-spam law)

Optional

  • An email verifier account (ZeroBounce, NeverBounce, Hunter, MillionVerifier)
  • A Claude or Gemini API key for AI-written first lines and reply drafts
  • Your CRM or sending tool, to push leads straight into it
  • Outlook, Yahoo and other mail accounts can send too; reading replies works with Gmail
