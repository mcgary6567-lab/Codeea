Emails, phones and website problems, found for you

Lead Agent scans each website for email addresses, checks the domain can receive mail, and lists what's wrong with the site.

  • No website, not mobile-friendly, no HTTPS, slow
  • Filters: has email, min rating, min activity, site problems

Personal cold email from your own Gmail

Write one message. Each lead gets their own version, mentioning the problem found on their site, sent a few a day inside the hours you choose.

Replies sorted, deals tracked

Replies, bounces and unsubscribes are read from your inbox and sorted. Interested leads move along the pipeline; you drag them to Won.
