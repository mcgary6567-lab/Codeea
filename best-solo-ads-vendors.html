Best place to buy solo ads: vendor, marketplace or directory?

There are three places to buy solo ads, and each suits a different buyer.

  • Straight from a vendor (like us, Igor or Petar). One list owner, one price, and you can talk to the person sending your traffic. Best when you want a funnel review and a guarantee from the person who sends the clicks.
  • A solo ads marketplace (Udimi is the biggest). Hundreds of sellers in one place, buyer ratings, filtered clicks and payment held until delivery. Best when you want to compare many sellers. See our Udimi comparison.
  • A solo ad vendor directory (like SoloAdsX). A list of sellers you then contact and pay yourself. Good for finding names; you do the checking.

Whichever you choose, start with a small order, track every click yourself, and read are solo ads worth it? before you spend.

How to vet ANY solo ad vendor before you buy

  1. Demand a Tier-1 commitment in writing — a percentage and a refund mechanism if breached.
  2. Ask for a recent buyer reference — talk to one human who's spent over $500 with them in the last 60 days.
  3. Insist on independent click tracking — never accept "we'll send you a screenshot from our dashboard."
  4. Get a funnel review before sending traffic — vendors who refuse are sending traffic to known losers and don't care.
  5. Read the guarantee terms exactly — "satisfaction guaranteed" means nothing; "free additional clicks if under 30% opt-in on a reviewed funnel" is real.
  6. Start with the smallest package — $100 to validate trust before $500+ to scale.
