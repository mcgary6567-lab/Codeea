About Ihsan Solo Ads

We built this service after getting burned by the same vendors that frustrated you. Here's our story, our values, and why thousands of marketers now trust us with their list-building.

Our Story

From Frustrated Affiliate to Trusted Vendor

Ihsan started in affiliate marketing the same way most people do — buying clicks, getting burned, and wondering why "verified" lists felt anything but. After hundreds of dollars spent on bot traffic and ghosted vendors, he started building his own email assets the slow, honest way: real subscribers, real engagement, real opt-ins.

What began as a personal traffic source quickly turned into something bigger. Friends asked to use the list. Then their friends. Eventually, Ihsan Solo Ads was born — a vendor built on the principle that the click count is just a number, and what really matters is whether those clicks become subscribers, customers, and long-term assets.

Today, we serve over a thousand affiliate marketers across the US, UK, Canada, and Australia, and we've kept the same core promise: every click is a real person, every campaign is reviewed by a human, and every guarantee is honored.

Our Values

Relationships Over Transactions

The clients who've been with us for three+ years didn't stay because of pricing. They stayed because we showed up — every time.
