How we publish results

We would rather you trust us a little less and understand the product a lot more, so every figure we publish says exactly what it is:

  • Backtests, not live results. Our performance figures come from the MetaTrader 5 Strategy Tester on real XAUUSD tick data, with the deposit, lot size and dates stated next to each number. There is no public live account yet, and we say so.
  • Weak periods are shown too. The longer tests include losing stretches of several months. They stay on the results page instead of being cut out.
  • You can check it yourself. The indicators are built into the EA, so you can run the same test in your own Strategy Tester and on a demo account before you risk money.
  • No promises of profit. No trading robot can guarantee profit or a prop-firm pass. Read our risk disclaimer before you trade.
