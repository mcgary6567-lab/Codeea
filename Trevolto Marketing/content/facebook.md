## 3. About (long description)

Trevolto is a desktop crypto-trading app for Windows and macOS. Connect your own
exchange account with read/trade API keys, choose a strategy preset (or bring your
own TradingView webhook), set your risk limits, and let it execute trades 24/7 —
hands-free, on your own computer.

Your API keys are encrypted and stored locally on your machine — Trevolto never
holds your funds and never takes custody. You stay in full control: pause, close
positions, or hit PANIC to flatten everything at any time.

Built-in tools: live strategy chart, backtesting, performance analytics, daily P&L
recaps, daily-loss limits and drawdown halts, and a Safe Mode for paper trading.

Trevolto is a tool, not financial advice. Crypto trading carries significant risk
and you can lose money. Only trade with funds you can afford to lose.
