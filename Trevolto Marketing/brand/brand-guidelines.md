# Trevolto — Brand Guidelines

Quick reference. Visual one-pager: `brand-guidelines.png`. Logo art and the
generators live in `trevolto/` (`make_logo.py`, `make_weblogo.py`, `make_social.py`).

## 1 · Logo
- **Primary lockup:** chevron mark + `TREVOLTO` wordmark (`trevolto/trevolto_logo.png`).
- **Standalone mark:** the chevron (`trevolto/logo.png`) — use when space is tight (app icon, favicon, tiny avatars).
- **Clear space:** keep at least the mark's width of empty space around the logo.
- **Web logo:** transparent 313×48 lockup at `site-trevolto/assets/img-01.png`.

## 2 · Colour
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Teal · primary accent | `#2dd4bf` |
| Cyan | `#22d3ee` |
| Light green | `#4ade80` |
| Smoke · background | `#1b1f24` |
| Panel / surface | `#23282e` |
| Elevated row / input | `#2c323a` |
| Text | `#ffffff` / `#e6edf3` |
| Dim text | `#9aa4af` |
| BUY / positive | `#3fb950` |
| SELL / PANIC / danger | `#f85149` |

**Mark gradient:** `#2dd4bf → #4a9eff → #a855f7` (teal → blue → violet). Do not recolour it.

## 3 · Typography
- **Headings:** Segoe UI Semibold (Windows) · Helvetica Neue (macOS) · DejaVu Sans (fallback).
- **Body:** same family, regular weight.
- **Wordmark:** bold, letter-spaced caps.

## 4 · Do & Don't
**Do**
- Use teal as the primary accent.
- Place the logo on smoke / dark backgrounds.
- Keep generous clear space around the mark.
- Keep BUY green and SELL/PANIC red in the product UI.

**Don't**
- Recolour the chevron gradient or the wordmark.
- Stretch, rotate, or add effects/shadows to the logo.
- Place it on busy or low-contrast backgrounds.
- Promise profit — always label figures *illustrative* and keep the risk line.
