Affiliate FAQ.

Everything you need to know before applying.

Who can become an affiliate?
Anyone with an audience interested in trading – content creators, signal channel owners, course sellers, traders with engaged social followers, bloggers, YouTubers. We approve serious applicants with relevant audiences. Spam tactics are not allowed and result in immediate termination.
How and when do I get paid?
Payments go out via Hotmart on a weekly basis. There's no minimum payout threshold – even a single $36 commission gets paid. We hold a 30-day refund window before commissions are final to account for refunds.
What if my referral asks for a refund?
If a buyer refunds within the 30-day window, the commission is reversed (this is why we hold commissions for 30 days). After 30 days, all commissions are final. Our refund rate is approximately 4%, so this is rarely an issue.
Can I run paid ads with my affiliate link?
Yes, with two restrictions: (1) you cannot bid on our brand keywords (NextTrade, Gold Scalpers, etc.) on Google or Facebook, and (2) you must include the legal trading disclaimer in any ad. Otherwise, paid ads on Twitter, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and email are all permitted.
How long is the tracking cookie?
30 days for all affiliates. If a visitor clicks your link and buys within that window, you get the commission – even if they returned through Google Search or another source later.
Is there a minimum number of sales required?
No. There's no minimum to stay active. However, accounts with zero clicks for 6 consecutive months are automatically retired. Reactivation is free – just email us.
Can I become an affiliate if I haven't bought the EA?
Technically yes, but we strongly recommend purchasing first. You'll convert significantly better when you can show the EA trading gold on your own account. Owners get a 50% affiliate-only discount on first purchase.
