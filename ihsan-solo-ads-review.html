What does Ihsan Solo Ads sell?

Ihsan Solo Ads sells solo ads: paid email broadcasts to an opted-in subscriber list, sold by the click. You send your squeeze page, we send an email that links to it, and you pay for a fixed number of unique clicks. If you are new to the model, start with What Are Solo Ads?

What happens if a campaign underperforms?

The rules are written on the refund policy page:

  • Before launch: cancel for a full refund, no questions asked.
  • After launch: problems such as a wrong geo split are fixed with free replacement clicks rather than a cash refund.
  • Low opt-ins: if a funnel we reviewed returns under 30% opt-ins, we send extra clicks free.
  • Deadline: email support@ihsansoloads.com within 7 days of delivery.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Published prices with no subscription.
  • Mostly Tier-1 traffic and 10% overdelivery on every order.
  • Free copywriting and a human funnel review before launch.
  • Clicks are trackable in your own tool, such as ClickMagick.

Cons

  • No cash refund once a broadcast has been sent; you get replacement clicks instead.
  • Not suitable for B2B SaaS, local services, luxury goods, CBD, gambling or adult offers.
  • Like all solo ads, most of the profit comes from your email follow-up, so you need an autoresponder sequence in place.
