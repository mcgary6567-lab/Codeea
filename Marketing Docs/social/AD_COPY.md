## ⚠️ READ FIRST — platform eligibility (this matters more than the copy)

Crypto / financial-product ads are **restricted on both platforms** — great copy
won't help if the account isn't eligible:

- **Meta (Facebook/Instagram):** ads for **cryptocurrency products & services**
  require **prior written permission / eligibility** (apply in Ads Manager).
  Without it, the ad is rejected regardless of wording. Avoid implying
  guaranteed returns; "get-rich" claims are disallowed.
- **TikTok:** **financial services** is a restricted/【prohibited-in-many-regions】
  category; crypto trading promotion is **banned or gated in most markets** and
  needs an approved advertiser + local eligibility. Check TikTok's policy for
  your target country before spending.
- **Both:** the **landing page** is reviewed too — keep the site's risk
  disclaimer visible (it is) and don't promise profits there either.

**Use only the ad-safe creatives for paid** (no profit figures). Keep the
profit-led set (`reel_01–10`, `reel.mp4`) for **organic** posts.
