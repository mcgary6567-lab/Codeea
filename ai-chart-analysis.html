What is Gold Scalpers AI?

Gold Scalpers AI is a free trading-signals app from Gold Scalpers. It scans Gold (XAUUSD), USDJPY and GBPJPY and gives one of three answers for each market: BUY, SELL or WAIT. A BUY or SELL comes with an entry price, a stop loss, three take-profit targets, the risk-to-reward ratio and a suggested lot size. It also reads a screenshot of your own chart. The app runs at app.goldscalpers.com and installs to the home screen of an Android phone or an iPhone. It shows analysis only: it does not place trades, and it does not need an account.

The only official address is app.goldscalpers.com. The app is not in Google Play or the App Store, so apps with similar names in the stores are not this one. On some screens the app's title reads “Gold Scalpers Agent”; it is the same app.

Under the signal

How the AI chart analysis works

Every scan ends in a decision you can check. The Analysis screen shows seven readings, and a setup has to pass them before the app calls it a trade.

  1. Higher-timeframe trendThe direction of the larger timeframe, with a bias score. In the Scalping style that is the M15 chart.
  2. MomentumRSI and MACD on the entry timeframe, and whether price is making lower highs or higher lows.
  3. ConfluenceHow many separate reasons agree, counted against a minimum. One reason is not a trade.
  4. Risk to rewardThe distance to the target against the distance to the stop. The app asks for at least 1:2.
  5. VolatilityWhether the current range is normal for the session that is open.
  6. Next newsThe next high-impact release for that market and how many hours away it is.
  7. Smart-money auditA score out of 100 for market structure, where price sits in its recent range, and whether liquidity has been taken.

What a passed setup gives you

The trade plan shows the entry, a stop placed behind structure, and three take-profit targets with the share of the position to close at each one. In the screenshot above that is 50% at the first target, 30% at the second and 20% at the third. The lot size and the dollar risk are worked out beside it.

When a reading fails, the app shows the idea as not valid yet and names the reason, for example a sell that sits too low in the range with no liquidity taken above it.

AI chart analysis from your own screenshot

  1. Open the app and tap Ask AI.
  2. Attach a screenshot of your chart from MetaTrader or TradingView.
  3. Ask a question, such as “Is this a valid buy?”

The answer uses your screenshot together with the app's live signals and news. You can ask about any pair.

A signal is a reading of the chart, not a promise, and AI answers can be wrong. Check the price with your broker and size every trade for a loss. To learn the ideas behind the readings, see gold trading strategies, the best time to trade gold and how US data moves gold.

Gold Scalpers AI: questions and answers

What is Gold Scalpers AI?

Gold Scalpers AI is a free trading-signals app from Gold Scalpers. It scans Gold (XAUUSD), USDJPY and GBPJPY and shows BUY, SELL or WAIT for each one, with an entry, a stop loss and three take-profit targets. It runs at app.goldscalpers.com and installs on Android and iPhone.

Is the Gold Scalpers AI app free?

Yes. The app is free and you do not need to sign up or log in to see the signals and the analysis.

Is it on Google Play or the App Store?

No. It installs straight from your browser in a few seconds: Chrome on Android, Safari on iPhone. There is no store download, and updates arrive on their own. Apps with similar names in the stores are not this app.

How does the AI chart analysis work?

Each scan reads seven things: the higher-timeframe trend, momentum (RSI and MACD), confluence, risk to reward, volatility, the next news event and a smart-money audit. A setup that passes becomes a BUY or SELL with a trade plan. One that fails is shown as not valid yet, with the reason.

Can it analyse a screenshot of my own chart?

Yes. Open Ask AI, attach a chart screenshot from MetaTrader or TradingView and ask your question. The answer also uses the app's live signals and news.

Does the app place trades for me?

No. The app shows signals and analysis. You place the trades yourself with your own broker. If you want trades placed automatically in MetaTrader 5, that is what the Gold Scalpers Pro EA does.

Does it work with my broker, MT4 or MT5?

Yes. The app only shows signals, so you can trade them with any broker on any platform. Prices in the app are indicative, so use your broker's price for the entry and the stop.

Which markets does it cover?

Gold (XAUUSD), USDJPY and GBPJPY, each in three styles: Scalping, Intraday and Swing.

How accurate are the signals?

No signal service can promise accuracy, and this one does not. Every signal shows its reasons and its stop loss, and the app says WAIT when the checks fail. Treat it as a second opinion and risk only money you can afford to lose.

Do alerts work on iPhone?

Yes, on iOS 16.4 or newer. Add the app to your Home Screen first, open it from there and allow notifications. Alerts do not work from a Safari tab.

Is Gold Scalpers AI the same as Gold Scalpers Pro?

No. Gold Scalpers AI is the free signals app. Gold Scalpers Pro is a paid Expert Advisor that trades gold automatically inside MetaTrader 5. Both are made by Gold Scalpers.
