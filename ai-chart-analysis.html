Questions

Before you install

Is the app free?

Yes. The app is free and you do not need to sign up or log in to see the signals and the analysis.

Is it on Google Play or the App Store?

No. It installs straight from your browser in a few seconds: Chrome on Android, Safari on iPhone. There is no store download, and updates arrive on their own.

Does the app place trades for me?

No. The app shows signals and analysis. You place the trades yourself with your own broker. If you want trades placed automatically in MetaTrader 5, that is what the Gold Scalpers Pro EA does.

Which markets does it cover?

Gold (XAUUSD), USDJPY and GBPJPY, each in three styles: Scalping, Intraday and Swing.

Do alerts work on iPhone?

Yes, on iOS 16.4 or newer. Add the app to your Home Screen first, open it from there and allow notifications. Alerts do not work from a Safari tab.

Where did the chart screenshot tool go?

It is inside the app now. Open Ask AI, attach a chart screenshot and ask your question. The answer also uses the app's live signals and news.
