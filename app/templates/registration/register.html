Consent & safeguarding

Online Quran College teaches children one-to-one over video. As the parent or legal guardian of the student(s) named above, you confirm that:

  • You are the parent or legal guardian and are aged 18 or over, and you consent to your child attending online classes.

  • We store your contact details, your child's name, age and academic progress to deliver the service, and we process them under our privacy policy. You may request access to or deletion of this data at any time.
