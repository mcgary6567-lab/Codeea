{% call ui.card("Contact masking") %}
  • Parent and teacher phone numbers are never shown in full in the admin UI; they render as masked values such as +44 7** *** *431.
  • Calls are placed through the in-platform masked calling service, so neither party learns the other's real number. Every call creates a CallLog entry.
  • Messaging runs through the college WhatsApp number and the portal. Off-platform contact is a policy breach for staff.
  • Students are identified by code (S-00001) in operational reports where a name is not required.
{% endcall %} {% call ui.card("Recording governance") %}
  • Opening a recording requires a stated professional purpose. {{ access_events }} access event(s) are on record.
  • Every open writes a RecordingAccessLog row plus a consequential audit event naming the viewer, the purpose and the IP address.
  • Recordings expire automatically at the end of the retention period configured in system settings.
  • Only QA, the teacher who taught the class and safeguarding leads can open a recording.
{% endcall %} {% call ui.card("Anti-poaching") %}
  • AI class monitoring raises a CEO-only flag when a possible off-platform contact exchange is detected.
  • Anti-poaching records are visible to the CEO and General Manager only; managers see conduct signals.
  • An AI signal is never sufficient on its own — a human investigation records the outcome before any action is taken.
{% endcall %} {% call ui.card("Child protection") %}
  • Background verification is a hard gate: an unverified teacher cannot be selected for a live schedule.
  • Classes take place in a college-managed room; join and leave times are recorded for every participant.
  • Anyone can report a concern from the Safeguarding page; reports escalate immediately to the safeguarding lead.
  • Parental consent is captured at registration and stored on the client record.
{% endcall %}
