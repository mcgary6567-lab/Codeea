# Ad-Copy Compliance Cheat-Sheet — Prometheus & Trevolto

**Purpose:** keep every landing page, blog post, email, and ad compliant with
Google Ads, Meta (Facebook/Instagram), and TikTok financial/crypto policies.
Applies to **both** sites. When in doubt, don't claim it.

**The one rule that covers 90% of cases:**
> Describe what the *software does* (automates, executes, monitors).
> Never promise what the *user gets* (profit, income, returns, wealth).

---

## ❌ NEVER say (instant rejection)

| Banned idea | Example phrases to avoid |
|---|---|
| Guaranteed outcomes | "guaranteed returns/profit/income", "your results are guaranteed", "make your money back" |
| No risk | "risk-free", "100% safe", "no risk", "can't lose", "zero risk" |
| Return multipliers | "triple your earning power", "double your money", "10x your portfolio" |
| Get-rich / life-change | "get rich", "rich quick", "life-changing", "financial freedom", "quit your job", "overnight" |
| Passive-income promise | "passive income", "earn while you sleep", "money on autopilot" (as an income promise) |
| Specific earnings | "$20,000 in 3 days", "5% profit your first day", "$X/month", "500% monthly" |
| Superlatives / proof of profit | "world's first", "proven strategy", "profitable bot", "grows your portfolio" |
| Compounding earnings | "reinvest your profits", "the magic of compound interest" |

## ✅ SAY instead (compliant reframes)

| Instead of… | Use… |
|---|---|
| Grows your portfolio | Trades on autopilot / runs 24/7 / automates your strategy |
| Guaranteed / profit guarantee | Satisfaction guarantee / money-back guarantee (on the **price you paid**, not on trading) |
| Risk-free | Fully refundable / try it in demo (paper) mode / no real money on the line |
| Proven / profitable strategy | Rules-based / built-in / battle-tested strategy |
| Passive income | Hands-free trading / hands-free automation |
| World's first | AI-powered / advanced |
| Triple your earning power | Level up your trading / sharpen your edge |
| Makes $X | Set up in minutes / fully automated / runs while you sleep |

## Testimonials — special rules
- ❌ No dollar amounts, no percentages, no earnings ("made $4,251", "up 5% day one").
- ✅ Allowed: ease of setup, time saved, hands-free experience, support quality
  ("Set it up in minutes — now it just runs").
- Meta/Google treat a testimonial's claim as **your** claim. A number in quotes is still a banned number.

## Numbers that ARE fine
- Product pricing and discounts ("$497", "save $300", "$29/mo").
- Competitor pricing in comparisons ("others charge $15–$110/month").
- Factual product specs ("connect in 10 minutes", "supports Binance, Bybit…").
- Technical/educational terms in blogs ("profit factor", "win rate") when defined, not promised.

## Blogs — you CAN discuss risky topics if you DEBUNK them
- ✅ "No bot can guarantee returns." / "'500% monthly' is mathematically implausible."
- ✅ Realistic, caveated expectation-setting ("a bot is a tool, not a money printer").
- ❌ Don't let the **call-to-action box** slip back into "proven, profitable, passive income."
  The CTA is an ad — hold it to ad rules.

---

## Required on every ad-destination page
- [ ] Visible **Risk Disclaimer** (or link): trading carries risk, past performance ≠ future results, not financial advice.
- [ ] Working links to **Privacy, Terms, Refund, Risk Disclaimer, Contact**.
- [ ] Real contact method (email + address).
- [ ] No fake countdown timers / fake scarcity (Meta & Google flag these).
- [ ] No auto-redirects or pop-ups that block content.

## Per-network gates (account-level — not fixable in copy)
- **Google Ads:** crypto **certification** required — registered/licensed entity + identity verification, applied from the Ads account.
- **Meta:** cryptocurrency ads need **prior written permission** (application with licensing proof).
- **TikTok:** crypto/financial ads are **prohibited or heavily restricted in most regions** — usually needs an approved/whitelisted agency; often unavailable regardless of page quality.

---

## Pre-publish 30-second check
1. Search the page for: `guarantee`, `profit`, `risk-free`, `passive income`, `proven`, `world's first`, `$`, `%`.
2. For each hit, ask: *is this promising the user an outcome?* If yes → reword. If it's debunking, pricing, or a defined metric → OK.
3. Confirm the legal links + risk disclaimer are present.
