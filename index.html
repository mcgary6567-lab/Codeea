Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you start.

What is Gold Scalpers EA?
Gold Scalpers EA is a fully automated MetaTrader 5 Expert Advisor for XAUUSD (Gold) — a pure 5-minute (M5) gold scalper. It enters when the Slope Direction Line crosses the 50 EMA (up = buy, down = sell), confirmed by the ZeroLag MACD. The stop loss is placed at the most recent swing high/low beyond the 50 EMA - real market structure - and break-even protection moves it to entry once the trade is in profit, so a winner cannot turn into a loser. Momentum Pyramiding can add to a winning trade as it runs (never averaging down), with every add-on sharing one basket stop. An optional Prop-Firm Mode enforces percentage-based daily-loss and max-drawdown limits from the real balance baselines. It filters ranging conditions, wide spreads, off-session hours and high-impact news, and a pre-flight self-check verifies the whole setup on attach. It opens, manages and closes trades hands-free while sending Telegram, mobile and terminal alerts.
Do I have to watch the charts myself?
No. The EA trades hands-free. Once it's on an XAUUSD chart with algorithmic trading enabled, it opens, manages and closes positions automatically. You can also enable Telegram and mobile alerts and use the dashboard's manual Buy, Sell, Pause and Close buttons.

MetaTrader 5, a broker that offers XAUUSD, and a computer or VPS that stays on during market hours. A VPS is recommended so the EA can trade 24/5 without interruption. You enable algorithmic trading and allow the EA's licence URL under Tools → Options → Expert Advisors.

What risk controls are built in?
Daily profit and loss limits, a max-drawdown guard, a max open-positions cap, a spread filter, and fixed-lot or percent-risk position sizing. Trading is still high-risk — always demo-test first and never risk money you can't afford to lose.

How long does setup take?
About 5 minutes: attach the EA to an XAUUSD M5 chart, tick Allow Algorithmic Trading, allow the licence URL (https://goldscalpers.com/licenses.txt) under Tools → Options → Expert Advisors, set your lot size, and it starts trading automatically.
Can I use it on a prop-firm challenge?
Yes — there is a dedicated Prop-Firm Mode. Switch it on and set your firm's numbers: the EA measures your daily loss as a percentage of the day-start balance and your total drawdown from the starting balance (or a trailing high-water mark, your choice). If either limit is breached it closes everything and hard-locks trading until you reload it. For firms that dislike multiple entries you can also switch pyramiding off and run one trade at a time. Always confirm your firm's current rules — no pass is guaranteed.
Does it use martingale or add to losing trades?
No. Momentum Pyramiding only adds to a trade that is already winning — the add-on orders sit beyond your entry and fill only if price keeps moving your way, and every one of them shares the same basket stop loss. It never averages down into a loser, and there is no lot doubling on losses. You can turn it off entirely for single-entry trading.
What happens if a winning trade turns around?
That is what break-even protection is for. Once a trade is far enough in profit, the EA moves its stop to your entry price plus a small locked amount — from that point the trade cannot close as a loss. On top of that, the step-stop pulls every stop tighter each time a new add-on fills, so the further the move runs, the more profit is already banked.
