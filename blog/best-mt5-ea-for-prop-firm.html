Best MT5 EA for Prop Firm Challenges: What to Check First

📅 June 26, 2026🔄 Updated November 14, 2026⏱ 8 min read
By Gold Scalpers Research Team · Firm rules checked on each firm's own pages, November 13, 2026

The best MT5 EA for a prop firm challenge is the one your firm allows and whose worst day fits inside the firm's daily loss limit. Most buyers check neither. They compare backtest returns, then find out after paying the challenge fee that the firm bans purchased EAs, or that the robot's normal losing streak is larger than the daily limit. This page gives the checks in the order that saves you money. New to EAs? Start with the best MT5 EAs of 2026.

Check 1: does your firm allow a purchased EA?

“EAs allowed” on a sales page often means “EAs you wrote yourself”. We read the rule pages of four large firms on November 13, 2026:

FTMOYesAllowed. One strategy may not control more than $400,000 across accounts, and funded standard accounts may not execute orders within 2 minutes of major news.
FundedNextUnder $50,000Only on MT4 and MT5 accounts below $50,000, for an extra fee. Larger accounts must be traded manually. EAs “designed specifically to pass Prop firm challenges” are prohibited.

Two of the four do not let a bought EA trade the account at all. The sources and the full limits are on the prop firm rules page, and smaller firms are covered in prop firms that allow EA trading. Whatever a comparison page says, including this one, ask the firm's support in writing before you pay.

Check 2: what the challenge measures

  • Daily loss limit, usually 3% to 5%. It is measured on equity, so an open losing trade counts before its stop is hit.
  • Maximum loss, usually 6% to 10%, fixed from the starting balance at some firms and trailing at others.
  • Profit target, usually 8% to 10%, with three to five minimum trading days.

The target is the easy part. An EA fails a challenge on the first two lines, and nearly always on the daily one. The arithmetic, with lot sizes for $10k to $100k accounts, is in how to pass a prop firm challenge trading gold.

Why most EAs fail challenges

  • Grid and martingale recovery. The equity curve is smooth until one trend runs against a stack of positions. The floating loss breaches the daily limit long before the robot “recovers”, and FundedNext lists grid trading as prohibited outright. See how grid and martingale EAs compare.
  • No stop on the trade. A robot that manages exits in software has no protection when the terminal or the VPS drops.
  • A daily limit that counts closed trades only. The firm counts floating loss. The EA has to as well.
  • Lots sized for a personal account. A drawdown that is acceptable on your own money can be twice a firm's maximum loss.
  • Trading through news and the weekend. Several firms forbid one or both on funded accounts, so an EA that passed the challenge can still breach afterwards.

Check 3: the seven things a prop firm EA needs

RequirementWhy it matters in a challenge
A daily-loss stop measured on live profit and lossIt has to act on floating loss, close everything and stay stopped until the next day
A maximum-drawdown stop, fixed or trailingIt has to match the way your firm measures the overall limit
A stop loss sent with every orderCaps the single-trade loss even if the terminal goes offline
No grid, no martingale, no averaging downProhibited at some firms and the usual cause of a one-day breach
A news filter and a session filterKeeps new entries out of the windows firms restrict
A position cap and a daily trade capLimits exposure in one symbol, which firms treat as a risk violation
A backtest you can reproduce, with the bad months shownThe worst drawdown, scaled to your lot size, is the number to compare with the firm's limits

The last row decides the lot size. Take the worst drawdown in the longest test you can run, and choose a lot that puts it at about half of the firm's maximum loss. How to backtest an MT5 EA shows the steps, and how to choose a safe EA covers the seller checks.

Frequently asked questions

Can you use an EA to pass a prop firm challenge?

At some firms. FTMO allows purchased EAs with conditions and FundedNext allows them on accounts under $50,000 for a fee. The5ers and FundingPips do not allow a purchased EA to trade the account. No EA can guarantee a pass.

What kind of EA fails prop firm challenges?

Grid and martingale robots, EAs without a stop loss on each trade, and EAs whose daily limit counts closed trades only. The firm measures the daily loss on equity, so floating losses breach the limit before any recovery.

What settings matter most for a prop firm EA?

A daily-loss stop about one point inside the firm's limit, a maximum-drawdown stop about two points inside, and a lot size that puts the EA's worst tested drawdown at roughly half of the firm's maximum loss.

What lot size should a prop firm EA use on gold?

Size it from the worst backtest drawdown, not from the target. For Gold Scalpers Pro on the Low drawdown preset that is about 0.04 lot per $10,000, which scaled the worst eight-month drawdown to about 4% of the account.
