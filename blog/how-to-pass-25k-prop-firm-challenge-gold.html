How to Pass a $25,000 Prop Firm Challenge Trading Gold (XAUUSD)

A $25,000 prop firm challenge is a popular funded size, and one where a lot of money is lost because traders start swinging for the target. This is the exact dollar math of a $25k gold challenge, the XAUUSD lot sizing, a pace plan, and the settings that enforce it. The Three Numbers of a $25k Challenge

  • Profit target +10% = $2,500 – reach $27,500 to pass.
  • Daily loss limit −5% = $1,250 – lose it in a day and you're out instantly.
  • Max overall loss −10% = $2,500 – drop to $22,500 and it's over.

You have twice the room to lose ($2,500) that you need for the daily battle, plus a hard $1,250 daily brake. It's a risk test with a $2,500 finish line.

XAUUSD Lot Sizing on a $25k Account

Risk 1% = $250 per trade, place a structural stop, and let it set the lots. One XAUUSD lot is roughly $100 per $1 move:

Stop distanceRisk per lotLots for $250
$2.00 (tight scalp)~$200~1.25 lots

$10.00 (swing)~$1,000~0.25 lots

The lot size falls as the stop widens – dollar risk stays pinned at $250. That's the 1% rule, and exactly what an EA computes every trade.

The Pace Plan: $2,500 in About 26 Trades

At 1:1.5 reward, each $250 risk aims at +$375. A 55% win rate gives +$95 expectancy per trade, so +$2,500 takes ~26 net-positive trades – a few weeks at a few setups per session. Use the entries from the XAUUSD scalping strategy.

“A $25k challenge isn't won on your best day. It's won by never having the day that breaches $1,250.”

Your Personal Daily Stop: $750, Not $1,250

Never let the firm's $1,250 line be your stop. Set a personal daily stop at 3% = $750 – three losers at 1% risk – leaving a full $500 buffer before the breach.
