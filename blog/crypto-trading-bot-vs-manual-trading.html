Where Manual Trading Wins

Judgment and context. A human can weigh a news event, a macro shift, or a chart pattern the bot wasn't programmed for. Discretion is a real edge in unusual conditions.Flexibility. You can trade anything, any way, and change your mind instantly — a bot only does what it's built to do.

Why Many Traders Use Both

The common answer isn't “either/or.” Many traders let a bot handle the mechanical, repeatable part of their strategy — the disciplined execution — while keeping manual control for judgment calls and larger positioning.

Prometheus fits this: the bot runs a managed strategy on autopilot, but your manual orders and account stay fully under your control.

Which Should You Pick?

Choose a bot if you lack the time to watch markets, you keep breaking your own rules, or you want consistent, unemotional execution. Choose manual if you have the time, enjoy the process, and trust your discretion. Many end up with a mix — and that's a perfectly good answer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a trading bot better than manual trading?
Neither is strictly better — they win at different things. A bot wins on discipline, speed and 24/7 coverage; manual wins on judgment and flexibility. Many traders combine the two, automating mechanical execution while keeping discretion for the big calls.
Do trading bots make more money than humans?
Not automatically. A bot's advantage is consistency, not prediction — it removes the emotional mistakes that cost manual traders, but it can still lose in the market. Results depend on the strategy and risk control, never on the bot alone.
Can I use a bot and still trade manually?
Yes. With a non-custodial bot your account stays yours — the bot runs its strategy while your own manual orders still go through whenever you want to step in.
