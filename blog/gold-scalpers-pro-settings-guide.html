Gold Scalpers Pro Settings Guide: Lot Size, Prop Firms and Sessions

📅 October 26, 2026⏱ 8 min read
By the Gold Scalpers developer · For version 4.50 · Checked 2026-10-26

Gold Scalpers Pro ships with the settings its backtests used, so the first rule is simple: attach it with the defaults, read the pre-flight report in the Experts journal, and change only what this guide tells you to. Everything below refers to version 4.50.

1. Lot size by account size

The one input every buyer must set is FixedLot. Size it from the bad stretch, not the good month: over eight months of real ticks the default setup’s worst drawdown was about a quarter of a $1,000 account at 0.01 lot (25.6%). The Low drawdown preset’s worst was about a tenth (10.3%).

3. Sessions and news

All four sessions are on (SessSydney, SessTokyo, SessLondon, SessNewYork), timed in your broker’s server time. Two cut-offs are on by default: no new trades in the first 2 hours of Sydney (SydneyOpenSkipHours) or the last 2 hours of New York (NYLateCutoffHours) – the thinnest hours of gold’s day. The news filter (NewsFilter) pauses new entries 30 minutes either side of high-impact USD releases from the MT5 calendar. Leave these on unless you have tested otherwise; see the best time to trade gold for why.

6. AI news trading (website edition)

HotStoryTrade (on by default) opens one trade per ForexFactory story that names a side with confidence 3 or more (HotStoryMinConfidence), with the 50 EMA on that symbol, a stop beyond the last two candles capped at 600 pips and a 1,000-pip target. These trades cannot be backtested. Set HotStoryTrade = false if you only want the measured rules; HotStoryLot lets you trade them smaller than FixedLot.

7. What to leave alone

  • Timeframe (M5), SlopePeriod (10) and EmaPeriod (50) – the backtests depend on them.
  • The MACD and chop filters – turning them off adds trades, mostly bad ones.
  • The licence and WebRequest setup – without https://goldscalpers.com in the WebRequest list the EA cannot confirm its licence.

FAQ

What lot size should I use with Gold Scalpers Pro?

0.01 lot per $1,000 matches the backtests, where the worst drawdown over eight months was about a quarter of the account. 0.01 per $2,000 roughly halves that. Below $2,000, use 0.01 with the Low drawdown preset.

What are the best Gold Scalpers Pro settings for a prop firm?

Turn on PropFirmMode (daily loss 4%, max drawdown 8%, inside a 5%/10% firm), choose the Low drawdown (NY only) preset, and size about 0.04 lot per $10,000 so the worst backtest drawdown is under half of the firm's limit.

Should I change the default settings?

Start with the defaults. They are what the published backtests used. Change lot size and the prop-firm block first; leave the entry, stop and target inputs until you have tested a change over several months.

How do I turn off news trading?

Set HotStoryTrade = false. The news row stays on the chart for information, and the EA only trades its measured Slope / 50 EMA rules.
