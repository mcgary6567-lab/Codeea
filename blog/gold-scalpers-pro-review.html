How it trades

The EA reads two lines that are drawn on your chart: the Slope Direction Line and the 50 EMA. A trade can open in two ways, and both are on by default:

  • The cross – the Slope line crosses the 50 EMA (up for a buy, down for a sell).
  • The turn – the Slope line changes direction, green for a buy and magenta for a sell, on the correct side of the 50 EMA.

Each signal must then pass every filter: price on the trade’s side of the 50 EMA, the ZeroLag MACD on the same side of zero, a chop filter that skips price hugging the EMA, a confirmation candle with a real body, a spread cap and a news pause. Only closed candles count, so a half-formed candle never opens a trade.

Exits. A cross entry’s stop sits beyond the last swing on the far side of the 50 EMA plus a 20-pip buffer; a turn entry’s stop sits beyond the last 3 candles. Once a trade is 300 pips ahead, the stop moves to lock those 300 pips. Targets are 1,200 pips for cross entries and 300 for turn entries.

On the website edition, an optional AI news reader reads the top ForexFactory story every 2 minutes and can open one trade per story. That part cannot be backtested, so it is not in any figure on this page; it can be switched off with one input.
