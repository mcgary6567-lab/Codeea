XAUUSD Volatility by Hour: What 62,079 Five-Minute Bars Show (2026, GMT)

📅 November 27, 2026 ⏱ 11 min read 🏷 XAUUSD · Volatility data
By Gold Scalpers Research Team

Most “best time to trade gold” guides describe the sessions from memory. We measured them instead: 62,079 five-minute XAUUSD bars from a live Exness MT5 server, 5 January to 18 November 2026, grouped by GMT hour, by weekday and by month. Here is what the numbers show.

Short answer: XAUUSD is most volatile from 13:00 to 15:00 GMT, the London and New York overlap, where a 5-minute bar averaged about $9.1 to $9.3 from high to low in 2026. The quietest hours were 04:00 GMT and 20:00 GMT, at about $4.1 and $4.4, less than half as much. The spread on the account we measured barely changed through the day, so the busy hours gave far more movement for the same cost.

That is the headline. Below are the chart, the full hour-by-hour table, the weekday and monthly figures, the same windows converted to New York, London and broker time, and exactly how we measured it, so you can check it or repeat it on your own broker.

What time of day is gold most volatile?

The chart shows the average high-to-low range of a single 5-minute XAUUSD bar in each hour of the day, in GMT. Every bar is a measured figure from the 2026 sample.

Four things stand out.

  • The overlap dominates. The 13:00 GMT hour averaged $9.12 per 5-minute bar and the 14:00 GMT hour $9.32, the two highest of the day. 15:00 GMT ($8.21) and 12:00 GMT ($7.15) are the shoulders either side. This is when US data prints and New York opens while London is still trading; our guide to how US data and the FOMC move gold explains why those releases matter so much to XAUUSD.
  • There is an early-Asia burst. The 01:00 GMT hour averaged $8.57, the third highest of the day and more than the London open. It coincides with the start of the trading day in Shanghai and the Tokyo morning. Gold can move in Asia; it simply does not keep moving for long.
  • The London open is modest on this measure. 07:00 and 08:00 GMT averaged $5.63 and $5.75, barely above the daily average of about $6.16. London matters for direction, but in raw 5-minute range it is well behind the overlap.
  • The quiet hours are very quiet. 04:00 GMT ($4.08) and 20:00 GMT ($4.37) were the lowest. The busiest hour moved about 2.3 times as much as the quietest.

Which hours are quietest, and why does 21:00 look odd?

Two windows are quiet for different reasons. 03:00 to 04:00 GMT is the late Asian session: Shanghai and Tokyo are around their lunch break, and Europe is not yet at work. 20:00 GMT is the New York afternoon after London has closed, when the US data for the day is out and positions are mostly set. The 10:00 GMT hour ($4.94) is a smaller mid-London lull before the US arrives.

The 21:00 GMT hour needs a warning. It has only 660 bars against about 2,700 for a normal hour, because the broker’s daily break falls inside it for much of the year. The break follows the 17:00 New York close, which is 21:00 GMT during US daylight saving and 22:00 GMT in winter, so the 22:00 GMT hour (2,023 bars) is short as well. Read both rows with that in mind, and avoid judging the rollover by its average range: what matters there is the spread and the gap in quotes.

A little, but much less than the hour of the day matters. Thursday and Friday were the busiest weekdays and Tuesday the quietest; the gap between them is about 10 percent.

What are these hours in New York, London and broker time?

The data is in GMT, which does not change with the seasons. New York and London do, so the same GMT hour falls at a different local time in summer and winter. Many MT5 brokers use GMT+2 in winter and GMT+3 in summer; the GMT+3 column below assumes a fixed GMT+3 clock, so subtract one hour if your broker is on GMT+2.

What this means for trading

Volatility data does not tell you which way gold will go. It tells you how far it tends to move, which is what sets your stop, your size and your costs. Some practical points:

  • Size stops to the hour, and the lot to the stop. A stop that is twice the average bar at 04:00 GMT (about $8) is smaller than one average bar at 14:00 GMT. If you trade the overlap, a wider stop with a smaller lot keeps the dollar risk the same while giving the trade room for normal noise.
  • The busy window is cheaper per dollar of movement. With a near-constant spread, the overlap gives roughly twice the range for the same cost. That is why most XAUUSD scalping strategies trade only these hours, and why the cost maths in our comparison of gold strategies rules out scalping the quiet sessions.
  • Quiet hours suit different tactics, not more of the same. Ranges of about $4 per 5-minute bar make breakouts less likely to follow through. If you trade late Asia or the New York afternoon, smaller targets and range-based setups fit the data better than trend entries.
  • Be careful around the rollover. The 22:00 GMT hour had the highest average spread of the day on this account, and quotes stop during the daily break. Avoid opening trades from about 21:00 to 23:00 GMT and check that tight stops will not be hit by spread alone.
  • Re-measure every few months. The average daily range fell by nearly two-thirds between March and October 2026. Settings that were right in one regime can be badly wrong in the next.

None of this is a trading signal or a promise of results. Knowing when gold moves makes risk easier to control; it does not make a losing strategy profitable.

How we measured this

  • Symbol and source: XAUUSD (spot gold against the US dollar), M5 bars exported from MetaTrader 5 on an Exness real-account server.
  • Period: 5 January 2026 to 18 November 2026, 62,079 bars in total.
  • Time zone: the server clock runs on GMT, so every hour on this page is GMT with no daylight-saving adjustment. Each bar is assigned to the hour of its open time.
  • Range: the high minus the low of each 5-minute bar, in US dollars per ounce. The hourly figures are the average and the median of every bar opened in that hour.
  • Spread: the spread value MT5 stores with each bar, converted to dollars and averaged by hour. MT5 keeps one spread value per bar, so this shows the typical spread rather than brief spikes, and spreads vary by broker and by account type. Yours may be wider or narrower.
  • Weekday: the average 5-minute range of all bars on each day of the week, by GMT date. Sunday covers only the reopening session.
  • Monthly daily range: the high minus the low of each server day with trading, averaged by month. Short days, such as the Sunday reopen, count as days, which pulls the monthly averages down slightly. January starts on 5 January and November ends on 18 November.
  • Limits: one broker and one account type; a dollar range is a different percentage move at different gold prices; and past volatility does not predict future volatility. Treat the figures as a description of 2026, not a forecast.

Frequently asked questions

What time is gold most volatile?

In this 2026 data, XAUUSD was most volatile from 13:00 to 15:00 GMT. A 5-minute bar averaged $9.12 from high to low in the 13:00 GMT hour and $9.32 in the 14:00 GMT hour, and 15:00 GMT was close behind at $8.21. That window is the London and New York overlap, when US economic data and the New York open land.

What is the quietest time to trade gold?

The quietest hours were 04:00 GMT, with an average 5-minute range of $4.08, and 20:00 GMT, with $4.37. Both are less than half of the 14:00 GMT figure. 04:00 GMT is the late Asian session before London opens; 20:00 GMT is the New York afternoon after London has closed.

How big is a typical 5-minute XAUUSD candle?

Across all 62,079 bars in the sample, the average 5-minute high-to-low range was about $6.16. It ranged from $4.08 in the quietest hour to $9.32 in the busiest. Medians are lower than averages in every hour, at $2.98 to $7.64, because a few very large news bars pull the averages up.

Is gold more volatile on some days of the week?

Only slightly. Thursday ($6.44 average 5-minute range) and Friday ($6.32) were the busiest weekdays and Tuesday ($5.86) the quietest, a gap of about 10 percent. The difference between hours of the day is much larger than the difference between days.

Has gold volatility fallen in 2026?

Yes, on this measure. The average daily high-to-low range of XAUUSD peaked at $164.73 in March 2026 and fell to $61.51 in October, about 37 percent of the March level. The first 18 days of November averaged $88.61. Volatility changes over time, so these figures describe 2026, not a fixed rule.

Does the XAUUSD spread change during the day?

On the account measured, the average spread recorded on each bar stayed between $0.17 and $0.18 for most of the day. It was highest in the 22:00 GMT hour, at $0.19, around the daily rollover. MT5 stores one spread value per bar, so short spikes inside a bar do not show in full, and spreads differ by broker and account type.
