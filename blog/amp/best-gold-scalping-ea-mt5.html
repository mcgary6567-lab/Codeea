Why a Gold EA Is Harder Than a Forex EA

Gold is the most-scalped instrument in the world, but the traits that make it profitable make it dangerous to automate:

  • Wider, variable spreads – gold's spread can triple during news and rollover.
  • Violent news spikes – CPI, NFP and FOMC move gold tens of dollars in seconds.
  • The rollover gap – around 22:00–00:00 server time, liquidity thins and wicks hunt stops.

A gold scalper needs filters a forex EA never bothered to build.

The 7 Features a Gold Scalping EA Must Have

  1. Fixed % risk per trade (0.5–1%) – start with the 1% rule.
  2. A hard daily-loss stop – turns a bad day into a small day.
  3. A max-drawdown guard – the account's seatbelt.
  4. A news filter – the biggest source of gold blow-ups.
  5. A session filter – skip the low-liquidity rollover.
  6. A spread guard – refuse to open when the spread is abnormal.
  7. A verifiable backtest – real tick data, several years, news months included.

Red Flags: Robots That Blow Up Accounts

  • Martingale & grid – a flawless curve until one trend takes the whole account. A curve with no drawdown is the red flag.
  • “No stop loss” – means the account is the stop loss.
  • Win rate as the headline – ask for expectancy and max drawdown instead.
  • Demo-only backtests – curve-fit to one period.
“A gold robot that never shows a losing trade isn't low-risk – it's saving all the risk for one catastrophic day.”

How to Verify Any Gold EA Before You Pay

  1. Tick backtest – 99% quality real tick data, several years. See the backtesting guide.
  2. Read the drawdown, not the profit.
  3. Check position sizing – bigger positions after losers = martingale.
  4. Forward-test on demo for 2–4 weeks.
