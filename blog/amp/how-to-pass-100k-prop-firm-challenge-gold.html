How to Pass a $100,000 Prop Firm Challenge Trading Gold (XAUUSD)

July 7, 202610 min read

A $100,000 prop firm challenge is the most popular funded size – and where the most money is lost, because traders see “$100k” and start swinging. This is the exact dollar math of a $100k gold challenge, the XAUUSD lot sizing, a pace plan, and the settings that enforce it. For general principles first, see how to pass a prop firm challenge on gold.

The Three Numbers of a $100k Challenge

  • Profit target +10% = $10,000 – reach $110,000 to pass.
  • Daily loss limit −5% = $5,000 – lose it in a day and you're out instantly.
  • Max overall loss −10% = $10,000 – drop to $90,000 and it's over.

You have twice the room to lose ($10k) that you need for the daily battle, plus a hard $5k daily brake. It's a risk test with a $10,000 finish line.

XAUUSD Lot Sizing on a $100k Account

Risk 1% = $1,000 per trade, place a structural stop, and let it set the lots. One XAUUSD lot is roughly $100 per $1 move:

Stop distanceRisk per lotLots for $1,000
$2.00 (tight scalp)~$200~5.0 lots
$3.00~$300~3.3 lots
$5.00~$500~2.0 lots
$10.00 (swing)~$1,000~1.0 lot

The lot size falls as the stop widens – dollar risk stays pinned at $1,000. That's the 1% rule on a six-figure account, and exactly what an EA computes every trade.

The Pace Plan: $10,000 in About 26 Trades

At 1:1.5 reward, each $1,000 risk aims at +$1,500. A 55% win rate gives +$380 expectancy per trade, so +$10,000 takes ~26 net-positive trades – a few weeks at a few setups per session. Use the entries from the XAUUSD scalping strategy.

“A $100k challenge isn't won on the day you make $4,000. It's won by never having the day you lose $5,000.”

Your Personal Daily Stop: $3,000, Not $5,000

Never let the firm's $5,000 line be your stop. Set a personal daily stop at 3% = $3,000 – three losers at 1% risk – leaving a full $2,000 buffer before the breach.
