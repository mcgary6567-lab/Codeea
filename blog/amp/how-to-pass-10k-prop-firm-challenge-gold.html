How to Pass a $10,000 Prop Firm Challenge Trading Gold (XAUUSD)

July 7, 20269 min read

A $10,000 prop firm challenge is a popular funded size, and one where a lot of money is lost because traders start swinging for the target. This is the exact dollar math of a $10k gold challenge, the XAUUSD lot sizing, a pace plan, and the settings that enforce it. For general principles first, see how to pass a prop firm challenge on gold.

The Three Numbers of a $10k Challenge

  • Profit target +10% = $1,000 – reach $11,000 to pass.
  • Daily loss limit −5% = $500 – lose it in a day and you're out instantly.
  • Max overall loss −10% = $1,000 – drop to $9,000 and it's over.

You have twice the room to lose ($1,000) that you need for the daily battle, plus a hard $500 daily brake. It's a risk test with a $1,000 finish line.

XAUUSD Lot Sizing on a $10k Account

Risk 1% = $100 per trade, place a structural stop, and let it set the lots. One XAUUSD lot is roughly $100 per $1 move:

Stop distanceRisk per lotLots for $100
$2.00 (tight scalp)~$200~0.5 lots
$3.00~$300~0.33 lots
$5.00~$500~0.2 lots
$10.00 (swing)~$1,000~0.1 lot

The lot size falls as the stop widens – dollar risk stays pinned at $100. That's the 1% rule, and exactly what an EA computes every trade.

The Pace Plan: $1,000 in About 26 Trades

At 1:1.5 reward, each $100 risk aims at +$150. A 55% win rate gives +$38 expectancy per trade, so +$1,000 takes ~26 net-positive trades – a few weeks at a few setups per session. Use the entries from the XAUUSD scalping strategy.

“A $10k challenge isn't won on your best day. It's won by never having the day that breaches $500.”

Your Personal Daily Stop: $300, Not $500

Never let the firm's $500 line be your stop. Set a personal daily stop at 3% = $300 – three losers at 1% risk – leaving a full $200 buffer before the breach.
