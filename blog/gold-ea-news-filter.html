Gold EA News Filter: Should Your EA Trade NFP, CPI and FOMC?

📅 November 27, 2026⏱ 7 min read
By the Gold Scalpers developer · News risk for XAUUSD robots · Checked 2026-11-27

Most gold EAs are built and tested on ordinary candles. A few times a month, US data turns those candles into something else: a jump, a spread that suddenly widens, and a fill nowhere near the price on your screen. A news filter is the setting that decides whether your robot is in the market when that happens.

Short answer: for most gold EAs, no. Around NFP, CPI and FOMC releases gold can move sharply within seconds while spreads widen and slippage rises, so a strategy built on normal candles stops behaving as tested. A news filter pauses new entries for a set window around high-impact events. Gold Scalpers Pro pauses 30 minutes either side of high-impact USD releases.

What is a news filter in a gold EA?

A news filter is a rule inside the Expert Advisor that reads an economic calendar and blocks new trades for a set number of minutes before and after scheduled events. A typical filter has four parts:

  • Which events – usually only those rated high-impact, so routine data does not freeze the EA all day.
  • Which currency – for gold that means USD, because gold is priced in dollars and reacts to US rate expectations.
  • The window – minutes before and minutes after the release time.
  • What it blocks – most filters stop new entries only. Some EAs also close open trades before news; that is a different, more aggressive feature.

Why does gold move so violently on US data?

Gold pays no interest, so its price leans heavily on what traders expect from US interest rates and the dollar. The releases that shift those expectations most are the ones a gold EA has to respect:

  • Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP) – the US jobs report, normally on the first Friday of the month at 8:30 am New York time.
  • CPI – the monthly US inflation figure, also at 8:30 am New York time.
  • FOMC decisions – the Federal Reserve’s rate decision at 2:00 pm New York time, followed by the press conference, and the FOMC minutes released about three weeks later.

When the number lands, liquidity providers pull back for a moment. Spreads widen, slippage rises, and price can skip levels entirely. A stop loss is an instruction to close at the next available price, not a guaranteed price, so it can fill worse than planned in that first rush. Our explainer on how US data and FOMC move gold walks through the chain from the number to real yields to XAUUSD.

Timing matters too. In US winter time, 8:30 am New York is 13:30 GMT, right inside 13:00–15:00 GMT, which were gold’s busiest hours in our 2026 measurements (Exness MT5 data, 5 January to 18 November). See the XAUUSD volatility by hour breakdown and the best time to trade gold.

How does a news filter work in practice?

Take an NFP release at 13:30 GMT and a filter of 30 minutes either side. From 13:00 to 14:00 GMT the EA will not open anything new, even if its entry rules fire. A trade it opened at 12:45 stays open with its stop loss and target in place; the filter does not move or remove them. At 14:00 the EA goes back to normal, and the first signal after that is taken as usual.

Two details catch people out:

  • Time zones. Calendars publish in one time zone, your chart runs on broker server time, and the US and Europe change clocks on different dates. A filter has to convert correctly, or it pauses the wrong hour.
  • Window size. A short window can still catch the first spike after the release; a very long one skips much of the trading day on busy weeks.

Do prop firms like FTMO restrict news trading?

Some do, and this is where a news filter stops being optional. When we read FTMO’s rules on 13 November 2026, funded standard accounts may not execute orders within 2 minutes of major news; Swing accounts are exempt. A 30-minute pause on new entries is far wider than that, but it only covers opening trades. A position that is already open can still hit its stop or target during the release, so read how FTMO treats that case on its own pages before you trade. Our FTMO page has the rule and its sources; firms change rules often, so check before every challenge. For the other features a funded-account EA needs, see the best MT5 EA for prop firm challenges.

For a scalper, the trade-off usually favours the filter: its edge, if it has one, comes from repeatable conditions, and a release is the least repeatable minute of the day. A strategy designed specifically to trade the news is a different product and should be tested as one.

How do you check that a news filter really works?

  1. Pick a known week. Choose a week with an NFP Friday or an FOMC decision and note the release time in your broker’s server time.
  2. Run it in the Strategy Tester. Test that week on real ticks, then open the trade list and check that no entry is time-stamped inside the window. Keep in mind that MT5’s built-in economic calendar cannot be replayed inside the Strategy Tester, so a calendar-based filter usually switches itself off in backtests. Gold Scalpers Pro works this way: its news filter reads the MT5 calendar, so it is off in the tester and the published backtests include trades taken around news releases. Live, it pauses new entries 30 minutes either side of high-impact USD releases.
  3. Watch it live on demo. During a real release, open the Experts tab of the Toolbox. A well-built EA writes a line when it pauses and when it resumes; if your EA writes nothing and still trades at 13:30, the filter is not doing its job.
  4. Check the clock change. Repeat the check in the weeks after the US and European clock changes, when hand-entered time offsets usually break.

Frequently asked questions

Should a gold EA trade NFP?

For most gold EAs, no. Around the jobs report spreads widen, slippage rises and price can skip levels, so a strategy built on normal candles stops behaving as tested. Unless an EA was designed and tested specifically for news, pause new entries around NFP.

How long should a news filter pause before and after news?

There is no single right number. A short window can still catch the first spike, while a very long one skips much of a busy day. Gold Scalpers Pro uses 30 minutes either side of high-impact USD releases, a one-hour window around each.

Does a news filter close open trades?

Usually not. Most filters, including Gold Scalpers Pro's, pause new entries only. A trade opened before the window keeps its stop loss and target, and a stop can fill with slippage during a fast move.

Does Gold Scalpers Pro trade during FOMC?

It opens no new trades from 30 minutes before to 30 minutes after high-impact USD releases such as FOMC decisions. Trades already open keep the stop loss set when the order was sent.

Is news trading allowed on FTMO?

When we checked on 13 November 2026, FTMO funded standard accounts could not execute orders within 2 minutes of major news; Swing accounts were exempt. Rules change, so read FTMO's own rule pages before trading.

Can I test a news filter in the MT5 Strategy Tester?

Only partly. MT5's built-in economic calendar cannot be replayed in the tester, so calendar-based filters, including Gold Scalpers Pro's, switch off in backtests. Confirm the pause on a demo account during a real release instead, by checking that no entry opens inside the window and reading the Experts tab.
