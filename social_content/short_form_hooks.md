## Production Tips

- **Hook on screen** = HUGE bold text, max 5 words, top third of frame.
- **Cut every 2–3 seconds** even on talking-head (jump cuts, B-roll of charts).
- **Captions always on** — most viewers watch muted.
- **Vertical 9:16**, 1080×1920, under 30 seconds for max push.
- **Music**: trending sound > niche sound. Check TikTok's "For You" page each morning for what's spiking.
- **First frame = thumbnail**. Make it shocking.
