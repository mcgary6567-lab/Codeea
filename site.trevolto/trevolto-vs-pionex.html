Custody: the biggest difference

Pionex is itself an exchange. To use its bots you deposit your funds onto Pionex, and the bots trade that balance. That's convenient, but it means your capital lives on Pionex's platform rather than an exchange you may already use.

Trevolto is non-custodial: it connects to your existing exchange account (Binance, Coinbase, Kraken, KuCoin, OKX, Bitget and more) through an API key with trade-only permission. Your funds never move to Trevolto and it cannot withdraw them. If custody is the deciding factor for you, our guide on whether AI trading bots are safe explains exactly how API keys work.

Cost

Pionex wins on raw price: there's no subscription for its bots and trading fees are around 0.05%. Trevolto is a one-time $267 for lifetime access. You're paying for a ready-made rules-based strategy, guided setup, and the freedom to keep funds on your own exchange — not for the bot software being unlocked month to month.

Strategy: many DIY bots vs one done-for-you

Pionex gives you a toolbox of 16+ bots (Grid, DCA, Rebalancing, Martingale and more). You decide which to run and how to tune them — flexible, but the responsibility is yours. Trevolto removes that decision: it runs one rules-based strategy, and you simply pick a risk mode. New to the categories? See bitcoin bot strategies explained.

Keep your funds where they are

Trevolto runs a rules-based strategy on your own exchange — your money never leaves your account. Try it in demo mode first, backed by a 14-day money-back and 60-day satisfaction guarantee.

Who should choose Pionex

Pionex is excellent if you want a free, all-in-one solution, you're happy to fund and trade on Pionex itself, and you enjoy choosing and tuning your own bots. Its low fees and bot variety make it one of the most beginner-friendly free options around.

Who should choose Trevolto

Trevolto is the better fit if you'd rather keep your funds on an exchange you already trust, you want a rules-based strategy handled for you rather than configuring bots, and you value guided setup plus a double guarantee. It trades flexibility for simplicity and custody control.
