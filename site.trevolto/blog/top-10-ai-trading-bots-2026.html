Top 10 AI Trading Bots for Crypto and Forex in 2026

By Trevolto  ·  Updated June 2026  ·  12 min read

The AI trading bot market has matured significantly in recent years. Dozens of platforms now compete for retail traders' attention, with varying approaches to custody, strategy, pricing, and supported exchanges. This guide evaluates the ten most relevant options in 2026 — ranked for beginners, with honest notes on pricing and limitations.

How we ranked these: We evaluated each platform on custody model (non-custodial preferred), exchange support, strategy flexibility, demo/trial availability, pricing, and overall ease of use for non-technical traders.

#2

3Commas — Best for Strategy Variety

Cloud-based Subscription Many bot types

3Commas offers an extensive range of bot types including DCA, grid, and options bots. Its marketplace lets you copy strategies from other traders. Cloud-based, which means setup is browser-only — but also means your API keys live on their servers. Subscription-based pricing from ~$37/month.

Best for: Traders who want variety and marketplace access over full local control.

#3

Pionex — Best Free Option

Built-in exchange Free bots Grid focus

Pionex is a crypto exchange with 16 free built-in trading bots. No separate subscription needed — the exchange monetises through trading fees instead. Grid bot and DCA bot are the strongest options. Limited strategy customisation compared to dedicated bot platforms.

Best for: Budget-conscious traders who primarily want grid or DCA strategies.

#4

Cryptohopper — Best for Signal Marketplace

Cloud-based Signal marketplace Subscription

Cryptohopper has one of the largest marketplaces for third-party trading signals. You can subscribe to signals from professional traders and have the bot execute them automatically. Subscription from ~$19/month. Supports many major exchanges.

Best for: Traders who want to follow external signal providers rather than configure their own strategy.

#5

TradeSanta — Best for Simplicity

Cloud-based Beginner-friendly Subscription

TradeSanta is designed for simplicity above all else. Setup takes minutes and the interface is very approachable for complete beginners. Limited advanced features — but if you just want a DCA or grid bot running quickly, it delivers. Subscription from ~$18/month.

Best for: Complete beginners who want the quickest possible setup with minimal configuration.

#6

Bitsgap — Best for Multi-Exchange Management

Cloud-based Multi-exchange Subscription

Bitsgap excels at managing multiple exchanges from a single interface. Solid grid and DCA bot offerings with a clean UI. Its arbitrage bot is a differentiator, though realistic arbitrage gains at retail scale are limited. Subscription from ~$29/month.

Best for: Traders running accounts on multiple exchanges who want a unified dashboard.

#7

Altrady — Best for Advanced Traders

Cloud-based Advanced tools Subscription

Altrady offers comprehensive trading terminal features alongside bots, appealing to active traders who also want manual trading tools in the same platform. Feature-rich but with a steeper learning curve. Subscription from ~$35/month.

Best for: Advanced traders who want a full trading terminal with automation features.

#8

Wunderbit — Best for Copy Trading

Cloud-based Copy trading Social features

Wunderbit focuses on social and copy-trading features, letting you mirror the bots of more experienced traders. Good for those who want to delegate strategy selection entirely. Subscription-based with a free tier available.

Best for: Traders who want to copy-trade rather than configure their own strategy.

#9

HaasOnline — Best for Professional/Custom Strategies

Local app Script-based Advanced

HaasOnline is a powerful locally-run platform for traders who want to write custom strategies in code (HaasScript). Extremely flexible but requires significant technical knowledge. Expensive — annual subscriptions start at several hundred dollars.

Best for: Developers and professional traders who need full programmatic control over strategy logic.

#10

Shrimpy — Best for Portfolio Automation

Cloud-based Portfolio rebalancing Index strategies

Shrimpy is less a trading bot and more a portfolio management tool — it automates rebalancing across multiple assets to maintain target allocations. Ideal for long-term holders who want automated diversification rather than active trading.

Best for: Long-term crypto holders who want automated portfolio rebalancing rather than active trading.

How to Choose Between These Platforms

The right bot depends on your priorities. Use this framework:

  • Maximum security (funds fully under your control): Choose a non-custodial, locally-run option — Trevolto or HaasOnline.
  • Lowest cost to start: Pionex (free) or Trevolto ($267 one-time, lifetime).
  • Most strategy variety: 3Commas or Cryptohopper.
  • Easiest setup: TradeSanta or Trevolto's connect-and-switch-on risk modes.
  • Full programming control: HaasOnline.
