How to Set Up Your First AI Trading Bot in 10 Minutes

By Trevolto  ·  Updated June 2026  ·  7 min read

Home › Blog › Set Up AI Trading Bot in 10 Minutes

Setting up an AI crypto trading bot sounds daunting if you've never done it. But with a ready-made platform like Trevolto, the process takes less time than you'd expect. Trevolto ships with its own proven, profitable strategy already built in — there's nothing for you to design, code, or predict. You simply connect your exchange, pick a risk mode, and switch it on. This guide walks you through every step — from creating your account to watching your first automated trade execute — with no jargon and no assumed technical knowledge. Trevolto is a web app, so there's nothing to download or install.

Before you begin: You'll need an account on a supported exchange (Binance, Bybit, KuCoin, OKX, Bitget, Kraken, or Coinbase) with some funded balance. If you want to test first with zero risk, you can use demo mode — no live funds required at all.

What You'll Need

  • Any device with a modern web browser — phone, tablet or computer
  • An account on a supported exchange (or just use demo mode with no exchange)
  • About 10 minutes

Step-by-Step Setup Guide

Step 1: Get Access & Create Your Account

Start your free trial or get lifetime access on Trevolto.com, then open your dashboard at app.trevolto.com and create your account — it takes under a minute. There's nothing to download or install; Trevolto runs in your browser and in the cloud. Turn on two-factor authentication to lock down your account.

Step 2: Create an API Key on Your Exchange

Log in to your exchange and find the API management section (usually under Account Settings or Security). Create a new API key with these permissions:

  • Enable: Read information, Enable trading (spot and/or futures)
  • Disable: Enable withdrawals — never grant this to any bot

Copy the API Key and the Secret Key. Most exchanges also let you whitelist IP addresses for extra security.

Security note: A trading-only API key cannot withdraw your funds. The bot can only place buy and sell orders — your money stays in your exchange account. Read more about bot security here.

Step 3: Connect Your Exchange in Trevolto

Open the Trevolto app and navigate to the Exchange Connection screen. Select your exchange from the dropdown, paste your API Key and Secret, give the connection a name, and click Connect. You should see your account balance appear within seconds.

Step 4: Choose a Trading Pair and Strategy Preset

Navigate to the Strategy section. You'll see options like:

  • Trading pair: BTC/USDT is the most popular starting point. ETH/USDT, SOL/USDT are also good options.
  • Timeframe: The 1-hour and 4-hour charts work well for the built-in EMA + RSI strategy.
  • Preset: Choose Conservative (lower risk, fewer trades), Balanced (default), or Aggressive (more trades, higher drawdown tolerance).

For your first run, stick with Balanced on BTC/USDT on the 1H chart. This is the most tested configuration.

Step 5: Run in Demo Mode First

Before touching real money, toggle the bot to Demo Mode. In demo mode, the bot places simulated trades on live market data — you see exactly how it behaves with no financial risk. Run it for at least a few days so you can observe entries, exits, and how the stop-loss and take-profit levels work in practice.

This is the most important step for beginners. Understanding the bot's behaviour before going live dramatically reduces the chance of making configuration mistakes with real capital.

Step 6: Review the Dashboard

The main dashboard shows you:

  • Open positions and their current P&L
  • Closed trade history with detailed entry/exit data
  • A live activity log showing every action the bot takes
  • Overall account balance and performance metrics

Spend some time here in demo mode getting comfortable with what the numbers mean before switching to live trading.

Step 7: Go Live When You're Ready

When you're comfortable with demo performance, go to Settings and switch from Demo to Live mode. The bot will now place real orders on your exchange. Start with a modest amount — many users begin with $100–$500 — and scale up gradually as you gain confidence.

Tips for Your First Week

  • Don't change settings every day. Give the built-in strategy at least 2–4 weeks to show its character across different market conditions.
  • Check the activity log, not the P&L, initially. Understanding why the bot is doing what it's doing builds much better instincts than just watching dollar amounts.
  • Use the Done-For-You setup call. Trevolto includes an expert setup call with your purchase — use it. Even experienced traders find these sessions cut weeks off the learning curve.
  • Never grant withdrawal permissions. Always double-check your API key settings on your exchange.

Common First-Time Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping demo mode and going straight to live trading
  • Starting with too much capital before understanding the bot's drawdown behaviour
  • Switching your risk mode after every losing trade instead of letting the built-in strategy play out
  • Running the bot on very small timeframes (1-minute, 5-minute) which tend to generate too many low-quality signals
