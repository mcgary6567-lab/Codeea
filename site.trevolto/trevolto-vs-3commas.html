Pricing: subscription vs one-time

This is the clearest difference. 3Commas is a software subscription. Its paid tiers are commonly listed from roughly $15/month (Starter) up to about $110/month (Expert), with discounts for annual billing. That recurring cost buys you a deep, flexible toolkit — but it never stops.

Trevolto is a single one-time payment of $267 for lifetime access (currently discounted from $497). There's no monthly fee. For a long-term user, a one-time license usually works out cheaper than a multi-year subscription — though if you only plan to trade for a month or two, 3Commas' low entry tier may cost less up front.

Strategy: build-your-own vs done-for-you

3Commas gives you the building blocks — DCA, Grid and Signal bots, SmartTrade order tools, and even an AI assistant that helps you assemble strategies. The flip side is that you are responsible for choosing, configuring and tuning what runs. That's empowering for experienced traders and overwhelming for beginners.

Trevolto ships with one proven strategy already built in. There are no bots to assemble or indicators to wire up — you pick a risk mode (Conservative, Balanced or Aggressive) and switch it on. If you want to understand the difference between approaches first, see our explainer on bitcoin bot strategies.

Safety & custody

Good news: both tools are non-custodial. You connect via an exchange API key that grants trade-only permission, so neither platform can withdraw your funds — your capital stays in your own exchange account. If custody and API safety are new to you, read are AI trading bots safe?

Ease of use & setup

3Commas is self-serve: you'll spend time learning the dashboard and dialing in settings. Trevolto includes a guided, done-for-you setup call and most users are live in around 10 minutes — see how to set up a bot in 10 minutes.

Who should choose 3Commas

3Commas is the better pick if you genuinely enjoy the craft of trading: you want to build multiple custom bots, run several strategies at once, connect a wide range of exchanges, and you're comfortable paying monthly for that flexibility. It's a mature, well-supported platform with a large feature set.

Who should choose Trevolto

Trevolto is the better pick if you want a ready-made, proven strategy, a single payment instead of a subscription, and a hands-free experience with guided setup and a double guarantee. It's built for people who want the outcome of automated trading without becoming a strategy designer.
