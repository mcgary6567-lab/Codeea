Pricing: subscription + marketplace vs one-time

Cryptohopper offers a free plan plus paid tiers commonly listed from about $24/month to $108/month. On top of the subscription, its marketplace strategies and signal subscriptions can carry their own recurring or one-off fees — so the true monthly cost depends on what you add. Trevolto is a single one-time $267 with no add-ons.

Strategy: a marketplace vs one proven strategy

Cryptohopper's standout feature is its marketplace — you can browse, subscribe to or buy strategies, templates and signals from other traders. That's powerful if you like experimenting, but it also means the burden of choosing what actually works falls on you. Trevolto takes the opposite stance: it ships with one strategy that's already proven, so there's nothing to evaluate or purchase. To understand the strategy landscape, see bitcoin bot strategies explained.

Both run in the cloud, 24/7

Trevolto used to be a desktop app — it's now a full web app that runs in the cloud around the clock, just like Cryptohopper. There is nothing to download or install, and it keeps trading even when your own computer is off. You log in from any browser on your phone or laptop to check on it. Both platforms connect to your exchange securely by API, and with both your funds stay in your own exchange account.

Safety & custody

Both are non-custodial: each connects to your own exchange via API with trade-only permission and cannot withdraw your funds. See are AI trading bots safe? for how this works.

Skip the marketplace rabbit hole

Trevolto's proven strategy is built in — no strategies to shop for, no extra signal fees. Try it in demo mode first, backed by a 14-day money-back and 60-day profit guarantee.
