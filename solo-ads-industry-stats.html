Top 5 takeaways from the data

  1. The funnel matters more than the vendor. Reviewed funnels average 42% opt-in; un-reviewed funnels average 18%. That's the single biggest performance lever in solo ads.
  2. Day 1 profitability is a minority outcome. Only 31% of first campaigns are profitable on day 1 — but 64% are profitable over 30 days. Plan for the 30-day window, not the 24-hour window.
  3. MMO and crypto education dominate ROI. 148% and 132% average 30-day ROI respectively. B2B SaaS and coaching underperform — wrong audience for the channel.
  4. Single-field forms beat multi-field forms 2.4×. Asking for name drops opt-in 8–14%; asking for phone drops it 30%+.
  5. Tier-1 traffic is worth the premium. $0.55–$0.75 Tier-1 clicks produce 40%+ opt-ins; $0.25–$0.40 mixed clicks produce 18–24%. Cost per subscriber favors Tier-1 every time.
