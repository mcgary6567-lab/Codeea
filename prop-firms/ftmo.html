FTMO rules for running a gold EA

By Gold Scalpers Research Team · Rules checked on FTMO’s own pages on November 13, 2026

FTMO is the firm most gold traders start with, and its rules are the template the others copy: a 10% target, a 5% daily loss and a 10% maximum loss on the 2-Step. It allows Expert Advisors, but the detail of how it counts the daily loss and what a funded account may do around news decides whether an EA survives there.

Purchased EA: allowed, with conditions

FTMO says it has no reason to restrict “discretionary trading, algorithmic trading or EAs”. For an EA you bought, two things matter: the $400,000 allocation cap per strategy, and the news and weekend rules that start once you are funded on a standard account.

How FTMO measures the daily loss

FTMO takes your balance at midnight CE(S)T and subtracts a fixed percentage of the initial account. Your equity may not fall below that line during the day, and equity includes open positions, swaps and commissions. On a $100,000 2-Step account that starts the day at $103,000, the line is $98,000. A floating loss counts exactly like a closed one, and a breach closes every position and ends the account.

What FTMO says about EAs

  • EAs are allowed, and a stop-loss is not mandatory (allowed strategies).
  • Maximum allocation: “$400,000 per trader or strategy (prior to any scaling)”. It covers identical or substantially similar trading across accounts. For a third-party EA FTMO warns that “you potentially run a risk of being denied the FTMO Account if you exceed the maximum capital allocation rule” (allocation rule).
  • Server limits: 200 open orders at a time, 2,000 positions a day, and no more than 2,000 server requests a day. A bar-based M5 EA is normally far below that; a stop that trails on every tick may not be.
  • Forbidden: exploiting price errors or feed delays, opposite positions across connected accounts, ultra-high-speed tools, gap trading, and over-leveraged one-sided bets (forbidden practices).
  • Martingale and grid are not named, but the risk rules on “substantially larger position sizes” and cumulative exposure in one symbol can be applied to them.

News and weekend rules

News. On funded standard FTMO Accounts and Instant Accounts, no order may execute from 2 minutes before to 2 minutes after selected releases on the targeted instruments. A stop-loss, take-profit or pending order that fills inside the window counts. Gold is targeted for the major USD events (non-farm payrolls, CPI, FOMC, the Fed rate decision). The Challenge, the Verification and Swing accounts are exempt (news rule).

Weekends. Funded standard and Instant accounts must be flat before any market break longer than 2 hours, which means no weekend holding. Challenge, Verification and Swing accounts have no such restriction (holding rule).

Gold on FTMO

Gold (XAUUSD) on FTMO: 100 ounces per lot, leverage 1:50 on standard accounts and 1:15 on Swing, commission 0.0014% of volume (FTMO symbols). Platforms: MT4, MT5, cTrader and TradingView. US clients are sent to a separate FTMO and OANDA site.

FAQ

Does FTMO allow Expert Advisors?

Yes. FTMO allows algorithmic trading and EAs on its challenges and funded accounts, subject to its forbidden-practices list, a limit of 2,000 server requests a day and the $400,000 maximum allocation per trader or strategy.

Can I use a purchased EA on FTMO?

Yes, but FTMO warns that a third-party EA can put you over the maximum capital allocation rule if other traders run it the same way, and the account can be denied on that basis.

Can an EA trade gold through news on FTMO?

During the Challenge and Verification, yes. On a funded standard account no order may execute within 2 minutes of major USD releases on gold, including a stop-loss or take-profit. Swing accounts are exempt.

How does FTMO calculate the daily loss?

From the balance at midnight CE(S)T minus a fixed percentage of the initial account. Equity, including open trades, may not fall below that line.
