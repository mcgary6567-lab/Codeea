FundedNext rules for running a gold EA

By Gold Scalpers Research Team · Rules checked on FundedNext’s own pages on November 13, 2026

FundedNext changed its EA policy in 2026, and most guides still describe the old one. A $100,000 Stellar account can no longer run an EA of any kind, including a tool that only sets the stop or the lot size. If you want to automate gold here, you are choosing among the small accounts.

Purchased EA: only on accounts under $50,000

FundedNext allows third-party EAs on MetaTrader 4 and 5 “for accounts below $50,000”, with an added EA usage fee. Accounts of $50,000 and above “must trade manually”, in the challenge and when funded. No EAs at all on cTrader or Match-Trader.

How FundedNext measures the daily loss

The daily limit is a fixed dollar amount: the initial balance times the percentage. Floating profit and loss, swaps and commissions all count, and the day resets at 00:00 server time (daily loss article). On a $25,000 Stellar 2-Step that is $1,250 a day. Once funded there is a further rule: no more than 3% of the account at risk at any moment, and a second violation cuts that to 1% (risk limits).

What FundedNext says about EAs

  • “Each EA or bot is required to employ a distinct strategy, avoiding identical trades across any accounts within FundedNext.” There is a $300,000 maximum allocation per EA strategy (EA policy).
  • “Using EA that incorporates third-party applications such as Telegram or WhatsApp is strictly prohibited.”
  • “EAs or bots designed specifically to pass Prop firm challenges are strictly prohibited.” FundedNext names several commercial products as examples.
  • Tools that only change the stop-loss, take-profit or lot size are classed as EAs and follow the same rules.
  • You may not pass with an EA and then trade the funded account manually, or the reverse (strategy restrictions).
  • Prohibited styles: grid trading, high-frequency trading, tick scalping and any arbitrage. Trades closed within 30 seconds must stay under 30% of your trades, and 200 or more trades a day counts as hyperactivity (prohibited strategies).

News and weekend rules

News. News trading is allowed. On funded 1-Step, 2-Step and Lite accounts, a trade opened or closed within 5 minutes either side of high-impact news keeps only 40% of its profit. Challenge accounts are exempt (news rule).

Holding. Overnight and weekend positions are allowed. Swaps count toward the daily loss (holding rule).

Gold on FundedNext

Gold on FundedNext sits under commodities. The rules page lists challenge leverage of 1:15 on the 1-Step and 1:25 on the 2-Step and Lite, while some product pages show lower figures, so read the margin on the platform before sizing. Commission on metals is 0.0016% of the notional per side (symbols and conditions). MT5 is offered, except to US traders.

FAQ

Does FundedNext allow Expert Advisors?

Only on MetaTrader 4 and 5 accounts below $50,000, and with a paid EA add-on. Accounts of $50,000 and above must be traded manually, and EAs are not allowed on cTrader or Match-Trader at any size.

Can I run an EA on a $100k FundedNext account?

No. From $50,000 upward FundedNext requires fully manual trading in both the challenge and the funded stage, and that includes tools that only manage the stop-loss or lot size.

Which EAs does FundedNext prohibit?

EAs designed specifically to pass prop firm challenges, EAs that use Telegram or WhatsApp, and any EA that trades by grid, high-frequency, tick scalping or arbitrage methods.

Is news trading allowed at FundedNext?

Yes. On funded Stellar accounts, trades opened or closed within 5 minutes of high-impact news keep only 40% of their profit. Challenge accounts are not affected.
