The5ers rules for gold traders: no purchased EAs

By Gold Scalpers Research Team · Rules checked on The5ers’s own pages on November 13, 2026

The5ers is one of the few large firms that still lets you run your own trading robot, but only your own. Its rules shut out every EA you buy, rent or subscribe to. If you trade gold by hand, or you write your own EA, the programs below are worth a look. If you want to run a purchased EA, choose another firm.

Purchased EA: not allowed

The5ers lists “Using an EA from a provider where the trader does not own the source code” among its prohibited practices. Gold Scalpers Pro is sold as a compiled file without source code, so do not run it on a The5ers account. We would sooner tell you here than have you lose an account over it.

How The5ers measures the daily loss

The5ers measures the daily loss from “the starting equity of the day OR the starting balance of the day (the highest between them)” at MT5 server time. If you carry a floating profit over midnight, the next day’s limit is measured from that higher equity, so giving the open profit back counts as a loss for the new day. Note the step down on larger accounts: a $100,000 High Stakes account has a 4% daily limit ($4,000) and an 8% maximum loss ($8,000), not the 5% and 10% most traders assume.

What The5ers says about EAs

  • The FAQ says you can use an EA as long as it does not copy signals, tick scalp, use latency, reverse or hedge arbitrage, trade at high frequency or use emulators, and the trader must own the source code (EA FAQ).
  • The prohibited-practices page lists “Using an EA from a provider where the trader does not own the source code”, and third-party EAs that leave many traders holding the same trades (prohibited practices).
  • The terms and conditions go further: automated software needs prior written approval, and the firm “prohibits use of any Automated Trading Software owned or developed by any third party other than the User” (terms and conditions).
  • Also prohibited: copy trading with other traders, bulk trading, one-sided bets, over-exposure and excessive server requests. A stealth (hidden) stop-loss is not allowed.

News and weekend rules

News. On High Stakes, no order may execute from 2 minutes before to 2 minutes after high-impact news. A pending order that fills inside the window counts, and the profit is removed. Holding a position through the release is allowed. Hyper Growth, Pro Growth and Bootcamp allow news trading except bracket strategies (news rule).

Holding. Overnight and weekend positions are allowed on every program.

FAQ

Does The5ers allow Expert Advisors?

Only an EA whose source code you own. The5ers prohibits EAs from a provider where the trader does not own the source code, and its terms require prior written approval for automated trading software.

Can I use Gold Scalpers Pro on The5ers?

No. Gold Scalpers Pro is sold as a compiled file without source code, which The5ers prohibits. Use it at a firm that permits purchased EAs.

What is the daily loss limit on a $100k The5ers High Stakes account?

4% of the higher of the day's starting balance or starting equity, and the maximum loss is 8%. Accounts up to $25,000 have 5% and 10%.

Can I trade gold news on The5ers?

On High Stakes no order may execute within 2 minutes of high-impact news, though you may hold through it. Hyper Growth, Pro Growth and Bootcamp allow news trading except bracket strategies.
