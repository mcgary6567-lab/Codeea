Prop firm rules for gold EAs, firm by firm

By Gold Scalpers Research Team · Rules checked on each firm’s own pages on November 13, 2026

“Prop firms allow EAs” is no longer a safe assumption. When we read the rule pages of FTMO, FundedNext, The5ers and FundingPips, the answers for a purchased gold EA ranged from yes with conditions to a flat no. This page is the summary; each firm has its own page with the program limits, the exact wording and the sources.

Main program means the first one listed on each firm’s page here, usually its 2-step challenge. A purchased EA is one you bought or subscribed to and do not hold the source code for, which includes Gold Scalpers Pro.

The firm pages

FTMO

Purchased EA: allowed, with conditions. FTMO is the firm most gold traders start with, and its rules are the template the others copy: a 10% target, a 5% daily loss and a 10% maximum loss on the 2-Step.

FundedNext

Purchased EA: only on accounts under $50,000. FundedNext changed its EA policy in 2026, and most guides still describe the old one.

The5ers

Purchased EA: not allowed. The5ers is one of the few large firms that still lets you run your own trading robot, but only your own.

FundingPips

Purchased EA: not allowed for trading. FundingPips draws a clear line between an EA you wrote and an EA you bought.

Three things to check at any firm

  • How the daily loss is counted. At every firm here it is measured on equity, so an open losing trade counts before its stop is hit.
  • What changes when you are funded. News windows, weekend holding and risk caps often apply only after you pass. An EA that was fine in the challenge can breach on the funded account.
  • Who else runs the same EA. Firms cap the capital on one strategy across all their traders. A popular purchased EA can hit that cap without you doing anything.
