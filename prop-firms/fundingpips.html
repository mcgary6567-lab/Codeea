FundingPips rules for gold traders: no purchased trading EAs

By Gold Scalpers Research Team · Rules checked on FundingPips’s own pages on November 13, 2026

FundingPips draws a clear line between an EA you wrote and an EA you bought. Your own robot can trade a challenge with proof of ownership. A purchased one may only manage trades you place yourself. For gold traders that makes FundingPips a manual-trading firm, and its rules for manual trading are detailed enough to deserve a careful read.

Purchased EA: not allowed for trading

FundingPips’ default rule is that third-party EAs are “permitted only when used strictly as a trade or risk manager”, and any other use “will result in denial of the evaluation or reward and closure of the account”. Gold Scalpers Pro opens trades by itself, so do not run it on a FundingPips challenge or Master account.

How FundingPips measures the daily loss

The daily limit is a percentage “of the higher value between the opening balance or opening equity for that day”. The system records both at 00:00 platform time, and equity may not fall by more than the limit from that baseline “at any point during the day, including floating losses from open trades”. The maximum loss is measured from the starting account size: on a $100,000 2 Step Standard, equity or balance may never touch $90,000. As at The5ers, a floating profit carried over midnight raises the next day’s baseline.

What FundingPips says about EAs

  • Default rule for third-party EAs: “permitted only when used strictly as a trade or risk manager. Any other use of a third-party EA will result in denial of the evaluation or reward and closure of the account” (Trading Conduct and Security Standards).
  • Personal EA exception: “If the EA is your own, developed by you, full automation is permitted with proof of ownership.” Accepted proof includes uncompiled source files, version history and an explanation of the logic on a call.
  • “A compiled binary on its own is not proof. Every trader has the compiled file after downloading any EA.”
  • Two account types differ: the 1K Instant account permits third-party EAs and trade copiers, and the Monthly Competition prohibits every EA.
  • The same article lists the forbidden strategies and the copy-trading policy. Read it in full before you trade any automated tool here.

News and weekend rules

News. In the evaluation there is no restriction on holding trades through news, but “purposely trading news” is prohibited in every phase. On a Master account, profit from trades opened or closed within 5 minutes before or after restricted high-impact news may be deducted; trades opened 5 hours or more before the release are exempt. On FundingPips Zero, holding, opening or closing within 10 minutes of restricted news closes the account (News Trading and Weekend Holding).

Holding. Overnight and weekend holding is always allowed in the evaluation. On a Master account it needs the Swing add-on. Zero does not allow weekend holds.

FAQ

Does FundingPips allow Expert Advisors?

A third-party EA is permitted only as a trade or risk manager. Full automation is permitted only with an EA you developed yourself, with proof of ownership such as the source code. A compiled file is not accepted as proof.

Can I use Gold Scalpers Pro on FundingPips?

No. It is a purchased EA that opens trades by itself, which FundingPips does not allow on its challenge and Master accounts.

How does FundingPips calculate the daily loss?

As a percentage of the higher of the opening balance or opening equity for the day, recorded at 00:00 platform time. Equity may not fall by more than that amount during the day, including floating losses.

Can I trade news on a FundingPips account?

Holding through news is allowed in the evaluation. On a Master account, profit from trades opened or closed within 5 minutes of restricted high-impact news may be deducted, and on FundingPips Zero trading or holding within 10 minutes closes the account.
