EMA cross & trend

After the cross, require this many consecutive green (long) / red (short) candles whose body ≥ the min ratio before entering (entry at the next candle's open). Set candles to 0 to enter right on the cross.

Stop-loss & target

Stop = the lower of {EMA21 − buffer%} and the recent swing low (mirror for shorts). Close the partial at the R:R target; trail the rest on EMA21 and exit fully on a counter-cross.

Safety guardrails (whipsaw & time)

If EMA9/21 cross more than max crosses within the window, new entries pause for the suspend hours (chop protection). Cooldown after stop-loss blocks a new entry on the same symbol for that many closed candles after any stop-out (0 = off) — stops the bot re-entering right where it was just stopped. Avoid daily close blocks new entries 23:30–00:30 UTC (existing trades keep their stops).

❓ Quick answers

Is my money safe?
Yes — Prometheus only places trades through your API keys. With withdrawals disabled on the key, funds can never leave your exchange.
Will an update wipe my data?
No. Your account, keys, trade history and licence live in a persistent database that survives every deploy. Only unsaved draft edits aren't kept.
Paper vs live?
Paper simulates fills so you can watch the strategy risk-free. Flip it off in Bot Setting → Scale-in / Execution when you're ready for real orders.
Forgot my password?
Use Forgot password? on the login screen — you'll get a secure reset link by email.
Can I control the bot from my phone?
Yes — connect Telegram in Settings, then message your bot /status, /pause, /resume or /closeall from anywhere.
Which IP do I whitelist on my exchange?
The Bot server IP shown at the top of the Exchange tab — the bot trades from our cloud, so your own IP will not work. IP restriction is optional; a trade-only key with withdrawals disabled is safe on its own.
Do alerts work when the app is closed?
Install it as an app and enable notifications for push alerts even with the tab closed. Telegram alerts always work.
Can I use my own strategy settings?
Yes — by default your bot runs our managed strategy, but you can request ⭐ Custom Strategy (VIP) access from the Bot Setting tab. Once we approve it, every parameter unlocks so you can tune your own higher-profit setup. It's built for advanced traders — test in the Profit Simulator first.
What is the signal-strength bar?
A live 0–100% meter on the Dashboard chart showing how close the strategy is to an entry — green for a building BUY, red for a SELL. At 85% it sends you a Telegram + app “get ready” alert seconds before the bot trades. It reflects the strategy only; your manual orders are unaffected.
What does “News trading OFF” do?
It pauses new bot entries for 1 hour before and after high-impact USD economic news (FOMC, CPI, jobs) to dodge volatility spikes. The header shows the next event and a red “Paused” flag while active. Open trades and their stops/targets are never blocked. Turn it back ON to trade through everything.
Can I stop the bot from trading on weekends?
Yes — in Bot Setting → Weekend pause, turn on “Don't open new trades on Saturday & Sunday.” The bot then holds new entries (and scale-in adds) all weekend, while open trades stay fully managed (SL/TP keep running) and your manual orders still go through. The weekend is UTC by default; tick “use my device's timezone” to follow your local Sat/Sun. A 🗓️ WEEKEND chip shows in the top bar while it's active.

🛠️ Troubleshooting — exchange connection & orders

Tap any issue to see the fix. Most connection problems are the API key's IP whitelist or Futures setup — not the app.
❗ “Invalid API-key, IP, or permissions” (Binance -2015)
Almost always the key's IP whitelist. If it connected before and suddenly fails, the bot server IP changed — open the Exchange tab, copy the 🌐 Bot server IP, and add it to the key's IP access restriction on the exchange (or set the key to Unrestricted). For Futures also: activate a Futures account, tick Enable Futures on the key, and confirm Futures is available in your region. Wait ~30–60s after saving, then Save & Connect again.
❗ “Order type not supported… use the Algo Order API” (-4120)
This meant a stop-loss / take-profit couldn't attach, leaving the position unprotected. It's fixed in the latest version — make sure your app is updated (redeploy). If you have an open trade that hit this before the fix, set its SL/TP manually on the exchange.
❗ “Restricted location” / HTTP 451
The exchange blocks your region/IP. If you're in the US, pick Binance.US in the exchange list; otherwise use Bybit, OKX, KuCoin or Bitget. Disable any VPN that lands in a blocked country.
❗ Spot connects but Futures is rejected
Futures needs its own setup: 1) open/activate a Futures account on the exchange (accept the agreement/quiz) — until you do, the key's Futures permission does nothing; 2) enable the Futures permission on the API key (re-create the key if you toggled it on after creating it); 3) make sure Futures is available in your region.
❗ Kraken Futures won't authenticate
Kraken Futures uses a separate API key from Kraken Spot. Create one in the Kraken Futures dashboard (not the spot key) with trading permission.
❗ Coinbase Futures won't connect
Coinbase futures run on Coinbase International Exchange (non-US). Use a Coinbase International API key, or switch to Coinbase Spot or Kraken Futures.
❗ Keys look blank / “Disconnected” after I log back in
By design — your key & secret are stored encrypted and never shown again, so the fields stay blank (you'll see “•••••• saved”). The bot auto-reconnects on its own; if it doesn't, just click Save & Connect. Still failing? Re-check the IP whitelist (see the -2015 fix above).
❗ No stop-loss was placed (Spot)
Spot markets have no stop-market order type, so a hard SL can't be auto-placed on spot. Switch the market to Futures for automatic SL/TP brackets, or set the stop manually. Take-profit still works on spot.
❗ Order rejected — insufficient balance / margin
The order is bigger than your available funds. Lower Risk % per trade or size, add margin/collateral, or reduce leverage. On futures, check funds are in the Futures wallet, not Spot.
❗ It shows “PAPER” / balance is 0 with a real key
Paper trading is turned on. Go to Bot Setting → Scale-in / Execution, uncheck 📝 Paper trading, save, then reconnect the exchange to trade live.
❗ Telegram: “chat not found” / “Forbidden”
Either the Chat ID is wrong or you never messaged the bot first. Get your numeric Chat ID (not the bot token), press Start in your bot's chat once, then re-save Telegram and hit Send test.
❗ Balance / positions look wrong or stale
Click Refresh. Make sure the key has read permission, and that the selected market (Spot vs Futures) matches where your funds actually are.
