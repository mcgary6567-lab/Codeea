Built-in strategy — EMA 9 / 21 cross on

Fast EMA crossing the Slow EMA triggers; a Trend EMA filters direction; N confirmation candles validate; the stop sits at the recent swing point. ON by default — auto-starts on connect.

Each selected pair runs the strategy independently. Concurrent open trades are capped by Max open positions (Settings → Guardrails).

EMA cross & trend

Long only when the candle is completely above the trend EMA and EMA fast crosses above slow; short is the mirror. Entry at the next candle's open.

Stop-loss & target

Stop = the lower of {EMA21 − buffer%} and the recent swing low (mirror for shorts). Close the partial at the R:R target; trail the rest on EMA21 and exit fully on a counter-cross.

Safety guardrails (whipsaw & time)

If EMA9/21 cross more than max crosses within the window, new entries pause for the suspend hours (chop protection). Avoid daily close blocks new entries 23:30–00:30 UTC (existing trades keep their stops).
