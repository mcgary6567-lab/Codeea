Prometheus

Automate your crypto strategy — right in your browser.

Connect your own exchange account, point TradingView (or the built-in strategy) at Prometheus, and it executes entries, stop-losses and take-profits for you — 24/7, with risk guardrails.

  • 🔑 Your keys, your exchange — Binance, Bybit, OKX, KuCoin, Bitget, Kraken, Coinbase. Trade-only, encrypted.
  • ⚡ TradingView webhooks → live orders with auto SL + TP1/TP2 scale-out.
  • 🤖 Built-in strategy — trade with no TradingView account needed.
  • 🛡️ Guardrails — daily loss limit, max positions, cooldowns, Safe Mode.
  • 📊 Backtest & analytics before you risk a cent.
