EMA cross & trend

After the cross, require this many consecutive green (long) / red (short) candles whose body ≥ the min ratio before entering (entry at the next candle's open). Set candles to 0 to enter right on the cross.

Stop-loss & target

Stop = the lower of {EMA21 − buffer%} and the recent swing low (mirror for shorts). Close the partial at the R:R target; trail the rest on EMA21 and exit fully on a counter-cross.

Safety guardrails (whipsaw & time)

If EMA9/21 cross more than max crosses within the window, new entries pause for the suspend hours (chop protection). Avoid daily close blocks new entries 23:30–00:30 UTC (existing trades keep their stops).

❓ Quick answers

Is my money safe? Yes — Prometheus only places trades through your API keys. With withdrawals disabled on the key, funds can never leave your exchange.
Will an update wipe my data? No. Your account, keys, trade history and licence live in a persistent database that survives every deploy. Only unsaved draft edits aren't kept.
Paper vs live? Paper simulates fills so you can watch the strategy risk-free. Flip it off in Strategy → Execution when you're ready for real orders.
Forgot my password? Use Forgot password? on the login screen — you'll get a secure reset link by email.
