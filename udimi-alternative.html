Why marketers switch from Udimi

From talking to dozens of buyers who tried both, the top five reasons people leave Udimi are:

  1. Seller roulette. One seller delivers 42% opt-ins; the next from the same filter delivers 18%. Hard to predict, harder to scale.
  2. Hidden commission. Udimi's marketplace fee is built into per-click pricing — direct vendors pass that saving back to you.
  3. No funnel feedback. Marketplaces sell clicks. They don't tell you your headline is killing conversions before you spend $300.
  4. Tier-1 percentages are seller-reported. No independent audit. With a single trusted vendor you build trust over multiple runs.
  5. Support is two-step. Issue → Udimi support → seller → maybe a resolution. Direct vendor = one person, one reply.

When Udimi is still the right choice

To be fair, Udimi makes sense when:

  • You want to test 5 different sellers in the same niche to find a favorite
  • You need a very specific filter combo (e.g. crypto-only with 70%+ T1)
  • You're spending under $100 total and want a quick first experiment
