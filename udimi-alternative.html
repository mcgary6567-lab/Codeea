Why buyers look for a Udimi alternative

  1. Results depend on the seller. With 1,600+ sellers, two orders in the same niche can perform very differently. You have to find, test and re-test sellers.
  2. You do the setup. On a marketplace you supply the ad copy and decide if your squeeze page is ready. Nobody reviews it before the clicks are spent unless the seller offers to.
  3. Prices add up at scale. Popular, highly rated sellers often charge more per click than a direct vendor's flat package.
  4. Scaling means repeating the search. When a seller sells out or changes their list, you start again.

When Udimi is still the right choice

To be fair, Udimi does several things well. Every seller passes ID verification, bad clicks are filtered automatically, your payment is held until the traffic is delivered, and undelivered clicks are refunded, according to Udimi's homepage. It also shows buyer ratings and "got sales" reports for each seller. If you want to test many lists or need a very specific filter, it's a good place to start.
