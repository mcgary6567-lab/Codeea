Card-brand logos for the checkout page.

Save official artwork here using these exact names:

    visa.svg        mastercard.svg    amex.svg
    paypal.svg      gpay.svg          applepay.svg

.png also works. A brand with no file here keeps its text badge,
so you can add them one at a time.

Get the official artwork from each network's brand centre rather
than copying it off another site — they publish free merchant
"accepted here" assets and set out how they may be displayed:

    Visa / Mastercard   their merchant brand centres
    American Express    amex brand hub
    PayPal              PayPal Logo Center
    Google Pay          Google Pay brand guidelines
    Apple Pay           Apple Pay Marketing Guidelines

Display them at their natural aspect ratio; the CSS caps height
at 20px inside a 32px badge.
