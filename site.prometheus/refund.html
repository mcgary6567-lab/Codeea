Refund Policy

In short: If Prometheus isn't right for you, request a refund within 14 days of purchase and we'll refund what you paid for the software and training. The money-back guarantee covers your purchase price — not money lost while trading.

1. The 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

You have 14 days from your purchase date to request a full refund of what you paid for access to the Prometheus software and training — no questions asked.

2. What's Covered

The 14-day guarantee refunds the price you paid us for access to the product and its training materials.

3. What's Not Covered

Trading losses are not refundable — cryptocurrency trading carries risk and any money lost in the market stays in the market. We also cannot refund fees charged by third parties such as exchanges, banks, or payment processors; requests made after the 14-day window; or amounts claimed through chargebacks rather than the process below.

4. How to Request a Refund

Email support@prometheusai.tech with the subject line "Refund Request." Include the email and name you used at purchase plus your order or receipt number, and a brief note on why you'd like a refund — your feedback helps us improve.

5. How Long It Takes

We aim to review requests within 3–5 business days. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method, and your bank may take a few extra days to post it.

6. Cryptocurrency Payments

If you paid with cryptocurrency, refunds are issued in the same cryptocurrency. Because crypto values change, the refunded coin amount is based on its value at the time of refund, which may differ from what you originally paid.

7. After a Refund

Once your refund is processed, your access to the software and training ends.

8. Contact

Questions about a refund? Email support@prometheusai.tech and we'll help.
