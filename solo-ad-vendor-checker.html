Frequently asked questions

How do I know if a solo ad vendor is legit?

Check for published prices, click tracking in your own tool, a stated Tier-1 share, a written refund or replacement policy and real buyer results. A vendor that ticks most of these is far lower risk.

What is a red flag when buying solo ads?

Refusing outside click tracking, no written refund policy, unrealistic promises of sales, and no way to contact a real person before paying are the most common red flags.

Does this checker store my answers?

No. The score is calculated in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.

What score should I look for?

Treat 9 or more out of 12 as low risk, 7 to 8 as test with a small order first, and below 7 as a vendor to avoid.

Should I still start with a small order?

Yes. Even with a good score, start with around 300 clicks, measure opt-ins and sales, then scale the vendors that perform.
