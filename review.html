What is Prometheus AI?

Prometheus AI is an automated crypto trading bot. Instead of watching charts and placing trades yourself, you connect it to your exchange and it runs a defined strategy for you — opening positions, setting stop-losses, and closing trades around the clock. It's built around a managed EMA-based trend strategy with fixed-percentage risk sizing, so you don't have to design or tune a strategy yourself.

How it works

The bot connects to your exchange (Binance, Bybit, Coinbase, Kraken and others) using a trade-only API key with withdrawals disabled. From there it repeats a simple loop: watch the market, decide when the strategy's conditions are met, place the order, then manage the trade with a stop-loss and take-profit until it closes. Because it's non-custodial, it can trade on your account but can never move your money off the exchange.

Who it's for (and who it's not)

A good fit if you: want hands-free, rules-based trading; keep breaking your own discipline when trading manually; prefer a one-time purchase to a subscription; and insist on keeping custody of your funds.

Probably not for you if you: expect guaranteed or life-changing returns; aren't willing to start in demo and learn how it behaves; or aren't comfortable connecting a bot to an exchange via API.
